Two request entry points create instances of shared_lookup. In a stripped HTTP/PostgreSQL adaptation, instruction dataflow alone assigned zero of six external-wait samples. Observed activation bindings assigned six. Keeping creation history led all six samples back to the correct request origin.

Tokio #6315 · Who spawned the delayed task?

In the controlled scheduler replay, a helper stayed in a worker’s LIFO slot while the spawning worker was blocked. A spawn hook and the helper-body sample recorded the same process-local task ID. The joined stack recovers the parent and launch site of that delayed helper.
