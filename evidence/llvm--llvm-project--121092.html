Issue findings

Local observation
On Clang 18.1.8 at -O2, four coroutine lifetimes reuse one frame address and resume on one worker. Symbolized GDB reports identical stacks and omits both launchers. Disabling assignment tracking restores the frame type with identical machine code. In that control GDB reads the frame, and the address and worker stack still agree across all four lifetimes.
What CoroPerf showed
The stripped resume entry saves its frame argument from rdi into rbx. CoroPerf recovers the argument from the captured registers and stack bytes, and matches each observation to a lifetime bounded by creation and destruction. The displayed joins 1 → 2 and 4 → 5 recover launch_profile and launch_inventory. The later joins 7 → 8 and 10 → 11 recover the launchers for the next lifetimes. Keeping only the first creation leaves the three later observations unattributed.
Scope and limits
The two-caller worker replay extends the published reduction on Linux x86-64. Frame pointers are omitted, and the app ELF has its symbols and CFI removed. Numeric fixture anchors select the capture points. The debug ELF labels addresses after reconstruction. Two batches verify 16 joins across both launch orders. Withholding the resume model or all creations leaves every sample unattributed.
