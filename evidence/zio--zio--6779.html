Issue findings

Local observation
On 2.0.0-RC6, a CSV quantity error at line 10 logged only runDrain at line 23 as a typed failure, while orDie and a throw from map showed the parser line.
What CoroPerf showed
The formatter took Cause.unifyFail, which rendered only the fiber stack. UnifyDie included the Throwable stack, and changing the terminal operation to runCollect moved the lone location to line 27.
Scope and limits
The report supplied no version, code, input or stack. This reproduces the logging symptom in a local component without establishing the reporter’s cause.
