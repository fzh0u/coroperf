Issue findings

Local observation
A local HTTP scrape reproduced the report’s 38 samples and HELP/TYPE lines byte for byte. The parser rejected the first histogram group that reappeared later in the output.
What CoroPerf showed
The collector inserted both views’ sums before their buckets and counts, and the exporter preserved that order. The fix accumulated each label group separately so its samples stayed together.
Scope and limits
Used the reported observations and 17 buckets with a real WSGI scrape. The original Django and Gunicorn configuration was unavailable.
