Issue findings

Local observation
Two senders blocked at the same doubleSend line, but goroutineleak marked only the sender whose channel the main goroutine no longer retained.
What CoroPerf showed
Swapping the retained channel swapped the flagged goroutine. Dropping the remaining reference raised the count from one to two, consistent with Go’s reachability check.
Scope and limits
coroPerf correlated the runtime’s labels with goroutine IDs. Reachability leaves future communication possible and does not establish intent or diagnose every deadlock.
