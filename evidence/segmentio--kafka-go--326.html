Issue findings

Local observation
One hundred writes took 100.54 and 100.55 seconds on two local three-broker clusters, versus 180.44 and 164.37 ms with Sarama. Setting BatchSize to 1 cut kafka-go to 139.48 and 115.50 ms.
What CoroPerf showed
The caller waited on its result channel while the partition worker waited about 1,000.1 ms for its timer before writing. Lowering BatchTimeout alone to 20 ms reduced the runs to 2.31 and 2.36 seconds.
Scope and limits
The reporter omitted exact versions and hardware. Loopback runs reproduced a roughly one-second write statistic, but the reported two-second interval was unspecified.
