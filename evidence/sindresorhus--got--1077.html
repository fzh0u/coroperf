Issue findings

Local observation
The public Got test read “ok” with a 200 and then “not found” with a 404. The HTTPError contained only the response callback and Node’s task queue, matching the reported frames.
What CoroPerf showed
Runtime object matches linked each GET, request, response body and error to its catch. Even on a shared socket, the two calls could be traced separately to viewInvoice and printInvoice.
Scope and limits
The reporter supplied a Node 13 stack but no app or command. Cross-stage matches used additional hooks and WeakMap comparisons.
