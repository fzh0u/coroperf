Issue findings

Local observation
In a GET–HEAD–GET sequence against one HTTP/1.1 server, the second GET opened a new TCP connection after a bodyless HEAD response omitted Content-Length.
What CoroPerf showed
The Python parser discarded the connection after HEAD. Applying the open PR’s parser change made all four test cases reuse one connection.
Scope and limits
The pool decision was observed directly. The order of flag updates in the native llhttp parser came from source inspection.
