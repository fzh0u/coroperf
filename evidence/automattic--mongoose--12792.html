Issue findings

Local observation
An invalid ObjectId produced a CastError without the getCat route frame when the handler awaited the query directly, while explicit exec() restored the frame.
What CoroPerf showed
The graph linked getCat’s await to the Query.then → Query.exec casting path, and a valid ObjectId on the same route returned an empty result.
Scope and limits
MongoDB 6.0.3 was connected, but the cast failed on the client.
