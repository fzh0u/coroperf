Issue findings

Local observation
In two uninstrumented runs, the client read the entire 4,519-byte PNG over HTTPS and printed only “main received 4519 bytes.”
What CoroPerf showed
The reader task’s stack and the returned data hash confirmed the read, while two positive controls showed where notification occurred on other read paths.
Scope and limits
The original icon was served locally. The fix comparison used Python 3.12.4, separately from the reported 3.8.6 environment.
