Issue findings

Local observation
With Selenium’s promise manager disabled, the 10-second wait failed without the helper in its stack, while skipping the wait exposed the helper in the failed click.
What CoroPerf showed
The helper was visible when the wait was registered, the condition was still false after 10 seconds, and enabling the promise manager restored the helper frame.
Scope and limits
The report omitted the full configuration and lockfile. The test used ES5, ts-node 3.3.0 and a locally proxied page.
