Issue findings

Local observation
With the reported schedule, 0.25.0 and the fix parent raised TypeError on the next heartbeat after crossing a month boundary. The fix commit, 0.26.0 and 0.26.1 retained the time zone.
What CoroPerf showed
Inside the same worker.main Task, the month-end branch produced a naive datetime that run_cron compared with an aware deadline before the job started. The fix preserved tzinfo.
Scope and limits
The reporter corrected the version to an unspecified older release. The local worker used the published schedule and a placeholder job.
