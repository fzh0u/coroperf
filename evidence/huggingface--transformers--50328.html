Issue findings

Local observation
With the reporter’s two public checkpoints and silence inputs, the regression model produced a raw score of −1.053481. The affected pipeline returned 1. On three music clips it also changed 11 of 30 multi-label decisions at a threshold of 0.5. All 30 decisions matched the independently calculated scores on the fixed build.
What CoroPerf showed
The affected parameter sanitizer filled in softmax when the caller omitted an activation. The same logits passed intact from the model forward call into postprocessing. After the fix, postprocessing read the checkpoint’s task type and returned the raw regression value or applied sigmoid separately to each label. Explicit activation controls on the affected release produced the expected values.
Scope and limits
The original report used a T4. These runs used an A100 with both original checkpoints, five builds, and three two-second music clips. The checks compare scores to the output layer and use an absolute tolerance of 0.001 for the batched music scores. Classification accuracy against human labels was outside this comparison. All recorded pipeline calls were synchronous.
