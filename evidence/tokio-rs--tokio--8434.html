Issue findings

Local observation
On Tokio 1.53.1, tracing the mutex handoff leaves two child futures pending and the mutex locked after 50 further parent polls. Stock Trace::capture shows the semaphore and mutex acquire stacks during the tracing poll and no leaf stacks once the children stop being polled. The two initial stock stacks stay the same when the launchers swap roles or submission order.
What CoroPerf showed
Each leaf stack is sampled without a child ID. CoroPerf assigns the semaphore sample to the child poll bracketed by records 6 and 8, and the mutex sample to records 9 and 11. The actual FuturesUnordered node in each interval leads back to the creation stack, recovering launch_profile and launch_inventory in the displayed run. Withholding those intervals leaves both samples unassigned.
Scope and limits
The displayed stacks are from a two-child extension of the official mutex handoff regression test on Linux x86-64. The scoped probe records FuturesUnordered node allocation and poll events. Stock and probed tests use Tokio 1.53.1 and 1.53.2. The merged change in 1.53.2 lets both children finish. The GitHub issue remains open.
