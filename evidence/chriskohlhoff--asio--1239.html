Issue findings

Local observation
When a timer resumed async_c, the physical stack ran through the scheduler, but GDB could still follow caller_ from async_c through async_b to co_spawn_entry_point.
What CoroPerf showed
The saved caller chain recovered the suspended calls, and a detached boundary would require a separate creation record.
Scope and limits
The timer experiment did not run Silkworm. Its original message stream, cancellation order, exact revision, and macOS arm64 build were unavailable.
