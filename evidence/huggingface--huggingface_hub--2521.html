Issue findings

Local observation
Two streams requested from the same loopback endpoint and left uniterated produced one client warning on the affected build and none after the fix.
What CoroPerf showed
Session and response identities tied the warning to submit_alpha and matched the independently recorded release order, and the shared URL and warning text could not distinguish the callers.
Scope and limits
The test used aiohttp 3.10.5, controlled the release order, and triggered garbage collection. Production frequency was not measured.
