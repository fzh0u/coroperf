Issue findings

Local observation
The original 10 × 20 fit succeeded. Adding a third dimension produced the reported broadcasting error on xarray 2024.11.0. When time and latitude had equal lengths, the fit completed and silently returned incorrect trends at all 24 locations in an NMC air-temperature sample with nonuniform weights.
What CoroPerf showed
Dataset.polyfit passed a weight array of shape (10, 1) into the multiplication of a (10, 20, 30) response. NumPy aligns these shapes from the right, placing the time weights on the middle axis. The fix supplied (10, 1, 1). With eight samples and eight latitudes, the affected release gave −1.208087 K/h at 75° N, 200° E. The independent weighted fit and the fixed release both gave +0.050764 K/h.
Scope and limits
The seeded public example ran on Linux x86-64 with the reporter’s Python, NumPy, pandas and xarray versions. The original used macOS arm64. The gridded temperatures come from the public NMC reanalysis example. We selected the sample and varied its weights as a diagnostic control. The NumPy fits ran synchronously.
