Issue findings

Local observation
For 10 rows and batches of three, two workers kept only rows 0–2 and 5–7 with drop_last_batch enabled, while one worker kept rows 0–8.
What CoroPerf showed
Each worker rounded its five-row shard down to three, dropping four rows in total instead of one. The proposal assigned six and three rows, and reopening the Arrow output confirmed all nine survived.
Scope and limits
Tested two multiprocessing start methods on Linux with Python 3.13. The report used macOS, and the proposed fix was still open.
