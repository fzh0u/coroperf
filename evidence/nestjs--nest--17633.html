Issue findings

Local observation
The original TCP event program sent its exception to a Subject with zero observers.
What CoroPerf showed
The trace followed evtRpc through the exception filter to Subject.error, where the subscriber count explained why no observer reported it.
Scope and limits
Used a local port. The Kafka branch of the related PR was untested.
