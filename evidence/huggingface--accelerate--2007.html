Issue findings

Local observation
With batch size 5, the author’s 9-item example returned only 0–3, and the 11-item example returned only 5 from the full batch 5–9; the fix returned 0–4 and 5–9 respectively.
What CoroPerf showed
The input batches and collective results were unchanged, and preserving _drop_last on the prepared loader prevented a second, incorrect trim for metrics.
Scope and limits
Tested with PyTorch 1.13.1 on CPU and two local Gloo processes. GPU training and NCCL were outside the comparison.
