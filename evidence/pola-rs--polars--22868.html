Issue findings

Local observation
With weights [1, 2], Polars 1.30.0 gave 1/3 for the first window and 1.33.0 gave 1, while the other four rows agreed. Grouped Parquet input showed the same first-window error.
What CoroPerf showed
Unit-vector controls showed the old implementation took the first weight and divided by the full weight sum, narrowing the defect to alignment and normalization in incomplete windows.
Scope and limits
Native stacks reached the rolling operator’s entry. Coefficient checks covered positive weights and inputs without nulls.
