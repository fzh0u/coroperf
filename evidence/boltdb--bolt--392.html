Issue findings

Local observation
The reporter’s on-disk test blocked in all four runs and passed in all four when the read transaction returned before waiting for the writer.
What CoroPerf showed
The reader held the mapping lock the writer needed at the 32,768-byte remap boundary; reopening the same-size file let the writer reuse pages 2 and 3 and commit.
Scope and limits
The report gave no Bolt revision, so the test used a contemporary commit. Cross-device index exchange was outside the test.
