Issue findings

Local observation
Two coroutines awaiting the same CompletableDeferred received the same producer-created exception with identical producer frames and neither await site.
What CoroPerf showed
The actual Job distinguished the two consumers and linked them to importOrders line 15 and refreshCatalog line 18 even when startup order was reversed.
Scope and limits
The reporter had omitted debug mode and supplied no application or versions. The two-waiter scenario was a local control.
