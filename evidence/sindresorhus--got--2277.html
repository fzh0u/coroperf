Issue findings

Local observation
Across two runs, only the download with the 200 ms connection budget failed, and all 13 error frames matched the report after path normalization while omitting the SDK and application callers.
What CoroPerf showed
Promise identities and the active timer identified the local caller and connect-phase abort, and switching the 200 ms and 2,000 ms budgets moved the failure to the other caller.
Scope and limits
The test used a published Electron downloader with locally chosen call sites. The original dependencies, domain setup, and timeout source were unavailable.
