Issue findings

Local observation
On Bun 1.0.0, printing an error after awaiting a timer in catch omitted the original synchronous throw frames, and printing without the delay retained them.
What CoroPerf showed
Separate debugger pauses captured the throw and the timer ancestry at print time, locating the two events independently.
Scope and limits
The pauses do not establish how the Error object lost its stack internally.
