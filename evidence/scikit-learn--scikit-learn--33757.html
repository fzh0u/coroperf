Issue findings

Local observation
For training inputs from 80 to 100, version 1.8.0 reported standard deviations of 7.7532, 43.6024 and 48.3014 at x=0, 90 and 100. The corrected values were 43.6024, 7.7532 and 9.1017.
What CoroPerf showed
Replacing only _bayes.py in 1.8.0 with the centering fix brought the entire curve into agreement with the posterior formula. Version 1.9.0 agreed, and both upstream regression checks passed.
Scope and limits
Dense floating-point inputs on a single CPU thread. ARDRegression was covered only by the shared upstream regression test.
