Issue findings

Local observation
For three ones and a NULL, both ranks followed the first window’s NULL ordering. Swapping the windows reversed both, and all 11 second-column ranks were wrong in a separate two-partition case.
What CoroPerf showed
The old comparison ignored null_order and EXPLAIN showed one WINDOW. The fix compared NULL placement, produced two WINDOW operators and restored the ranks.
Scope and limits
The real engine ran on Linux x86-64 rather than the reported macOS ARM system. The captured stacks were synchronous.
