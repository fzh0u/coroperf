Issue findings

Local observation
The reported IN query for IDs 1, 3 and 4 failed at a literal square bracket with SQLSTATE 42601, while the fix commit and 0.5.1 returned all three IDs.
What CoroPerf showed
Enabling render_postcompile changed one list parameter into three scalars, as confirmed by the server log; reversing submission order left the error with the IN query.
Scope and limits
The local aiopg/psycopg2 fixture supplied five rows. Only the additional concurrent control gave the read its own task.
