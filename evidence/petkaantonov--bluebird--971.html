Issue findings

Local observation
Nested fromCallback calls produced a forgotten-return warning without the corresponding creation stack, while the Promise-constructor control preserved more origin frames.
What CoroPerf showed
The returned Promise had no _trace, and its identity matched the object later seen in _warn, locating the missing trace on the fromCallback path.
Scope and limits
The constructor was a separate control. Imported native stacks did not establish coroutine identity.
