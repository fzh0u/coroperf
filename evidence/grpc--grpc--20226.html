Issue findings

Local observation
With grpcio 1.21.1, all ten initial calls went to whichever backend became ready first, whereas the next ten reached one backend each once all ten were ready.
What CoroPerf showed
Every call reached the round-robin picker, but it had just one eligible backend during the first burst and ten during the second. Picker logs matched server receipts.
Scope and limits
The replay used ten local HTTP/2 servers and static IPv4 addresses. The post omitted its server implementation, DNS settings and startup timing.
