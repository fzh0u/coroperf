Issue findings

Local observation
In 3.5.3, both featureA and featureB produced an interceptor stack showing the testFuture closure without identifying which feature called it.
What CoroPerf showed
The stack reached the closure through CInterceptor.onRequest, Future.forEach and doWhile. 3.14.0 restored a separate testFuture await frame but still omitted both feature entry points.
Scope and limits
The two features ran separately. The affected stack identifies the Future boundary only.
