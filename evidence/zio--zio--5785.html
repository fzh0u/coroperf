Issue findings

Local observation
In 1.0.12, a Cause logged inside an effect showed the parse failure but omitted externalRequestHandler. In 2.0.18, the internal log likewise omitted foo and bar without propagated context.
What CoroPerf showed
In two runs of the proposed example, ZLogger retrieved the StackTrace captured by fromJava from FiberRefs and passed that same object to Cause.traced, restoring foo and bar.
Scope and limits
The original logging APIs were private. The controls cover one runtime boundary and an unmerged proposal, PR #8509.
