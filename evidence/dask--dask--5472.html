Issue findings

Local observation
The reported 4×5 input produced chunks ((5,), (5,)) and a ValueError before the fix, while afterward it returned 20, matching NumPy.
What CoroPerf showed
The old path expanded axes=2 as (1,0) on the left and (0,1) on the right, while correcting the order also changed a square control’s sum from 69 to 70.
Scope and limits
The reporter did not pin Dask. Tests covered 2.5.2 and the commits around the merge. The block-product control used synchronous scheduling.
