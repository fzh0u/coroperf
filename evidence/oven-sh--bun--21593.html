Issue findings

Local observation
With the reporter’s error class on Bun 1.2.19, four real Redis messages alternated between 10 and 1 displayed frames. 1.4.4 showed 10 each time.
What CoroPerf showed
The same error’s .stack string had 10 frames, but Bun.inspect showed only the constructor after that read. Without it, handleMessageReply and the Redis decoder remained visible.
Scope and limits
The original app, messages and lockfile were unavailable. The replay did not identify the intermittent reader or reproduce the reported change of error name.
