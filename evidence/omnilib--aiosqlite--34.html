Issue findings

Local observation
For 120,000 rows, median iteration took 4.733 s on the old build, 0.123 s in batches of 250, and 0.071 s with sqlite3. On 0.16.0 iteration took 0.203 s but explicit fetchone still took 4.667 s.
What CoroPerf showed
The slow task repeatedly entered __anext__ → fetchone → _execute, and independent worker counts fell from 120,001 handoffs to 1,876 or 481 with chunks of 64 or 250, preserving the checksum.
Scope and limits
The original schema and data were unavailable. These are local Linux timings on a disk database. The reported 15× ratio came from macOS.
