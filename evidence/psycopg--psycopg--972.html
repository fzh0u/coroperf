Issue findings

Local observation
In the upstream LISTEN/NOTIFY regression, the 3.2.3 generator received foo/1 but the registered callback set_n2 left its result as None; the fix passed the same test.
What CoroPerf showed
The sender PID and payload identified the same notification in the old task’s generator branch and in the fixed task, which also invoked notify_handler and selected set_n2.
Scope and limits
Task identity was captured in the pure Python driver on Linux over a Unix socket. The related report used macOS.
