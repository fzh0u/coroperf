Issue findings

Local observation
The released broker received three distinct PostgreSQL notifications on two listening connections. All six callbacks raised NoEventLoopError. A scheduled job also missed its due time during the four-second observation. After a PostgreSQL restart, the schedule still carried its original due time and the job had written zero completion receipts. With the upstream one-line listener change, the job wrote its receipt in both batches.
What CoroPerf showed
asyncpg dispatched the synchronous listener on a running loop with no current task. Under an unmarked context, sniffio could not identify the backend when the listener asked AnyIO to start a delivery task. The exception stacks show the call through TaskGroup.start_soon and TaskHandle to get_async_backend. coroPerf also matched the specific scheduler coroutine created by AsyncScheduler.run_until_stopped to its Event.wait after the job was due. The same wait appears after a successful job, so the SQL receipt and schedule times establish which outcome occurred.
Scope and limits
The local test corrected two API calls in the posted snippet. PostgreSQL 14.13 ran on a Unix socket. Explicitly marking the context as asyncio restored delivery on the released code. Removing sniffio restored delivery for the standalone broker. This version of the SQLAlchemy data store imports sniffio and raised ModuleNotFoundError in that environment.
