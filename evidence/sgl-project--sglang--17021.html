Issue findings

Local observation
All five Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct runs completed 100 three-image requests each. The baseline and instrumented high-concurrency runs peaked at 100 in flight, with median times to first text of 28.071 and 27.550 seconds.
What CoroPerf showed
At high concurrency the median gap from tokenization to the first scheduler ID was 23.987 s, versus 4.166 ms from ID to text. Five of 100 latter gaps exceeded 1 s, and 4.513 s of the longest fell between detokenizer send and frontend Future completion.
Scope and limits
One run per condition on one A100 with Triton and overlap disabled. The original FA3 deployment and inputs were unavailable, and the production cause remains unconfirmed.
