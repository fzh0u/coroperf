Issue findings

Local observation
On the six-row example, Dask 2022.2.0 failed 48 of 48 runs: rows indexed 1 and 2 received 30 and 40 where both should have been missing.
What CoroPerf showed
The disk shuffle delivered one group in index order 5, 1, 3 before pandas shifted it. Sorting the index in the fix also eliminated 20 lag and 18 lead errors in a reversed-partition control.
Scope and limits
The original used macOS. The Linux replay pinned pandas 1.4.1. Partition order was controlled only in the separate 96-row experiment.
