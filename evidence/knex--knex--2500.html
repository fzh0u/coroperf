Issue findings

Local observation
For the same missing userss table, Bluebird’s then stack retained line 18 of test, while await retained three previous-event sections and lost test.
What CoroPerf showed
The Promise delivered to unhandledRejection matched its async_hooks creation in test. A separate configuration control restored both caller stacks when the builder’s wrapper also enabled asyncStackTraces.
Scope and limits
The graph identified the function entry only. The later commenter’s application was unavailable.
