Issue findings

Local observation
In the reporter’s Starlette app, a client disconnect cancelled a background task that was still running.
What CoroPerf showed
The affected task ran through BaseHTTPMiddleware into the background _sleep call, where it received cancellation. The linked fix removed that downstream cancellation.
Scope and limits
Verified with the supplied middleware and a local connection.
