Issue findings

Local observation
The original ValidationError on 8.12.1 omitted runDemo’s doc.save line, while 9.0.0 retained it and passed all 15 upstream stack assertions versus 3 on the older version.
What CoroPerf showed
The creation stack connected runDemo line 22 to validation through model.save and its pre-save hooks, although the error itself had only four frames and began at Document.invalidate.
Scope and limits
The old printer could already show the caller. Tests used a separate database and a 20-frame limit. The version comparison spans multiple changes.
