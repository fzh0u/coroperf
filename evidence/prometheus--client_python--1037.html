Issue findings

Local observation
The old client evaluated Timestamp(2,5) > Timestamp(3,4) as true, so its OpenMetrics parser rejected two increasing gauge timestamps that the fixed version accepted.
What CoroPerf showed
The old comparison checked 5 > 4 even after 2 > 3 was false, while the fix compares nanoseconds only when the seconds are equal.
Scope and limits
The report gave no exact version. The comparison used 0.20.0, the commits around the fix, and valid nonnegative timestamps.
