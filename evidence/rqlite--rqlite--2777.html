Issue findings

Local observation
Exporting only t1 on v10.3.2 reproduced the reported SQL byte for byte. Restoring it into empty SQLite failed on two indexes and a trigger belonging to unselected tables.
What CoroPerf showed
DB.Dump received the correct selection, but the three extra objects matched the failing SQL lines. The fix filtered by tbl_name and retained t1’s own index and trigger.
Scope and limits
Adjacent releases on a single Raft leader. The fixed dump still included two views that could be created but could not be read without t2.
