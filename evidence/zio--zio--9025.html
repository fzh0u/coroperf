Issue findings

Local observation
On 2.1.7, the FiberFailure printed at getOrThrow omitted Test.call.
What CoroPerf showed
The JVM stack still contained getOrThrowFiberFailure, Test.subcall, and Test.call when the exception was constructed, and PR #9386 added the JVM call stack at that boundary.
Scope and limits
The original app and a 2.1.19 control used the same input. The application frames were captured during exception construction.
