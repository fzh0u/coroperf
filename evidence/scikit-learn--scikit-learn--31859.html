Issue findings

Local observation
On Iris with 1.7.1, two one-step fits matched the feature coefficients of one two-step fit, yet 49 of 150 predicted classes differed and the first five rows had a maximum probability difference of 0.071784.
What CoroPerf showed
The fix subtracted the last intercept from all three to preserve relative log odds; both the patch alone and 1.7.2 matched the two-step reference, improving regression checks from 5/8 to 8/8.
Scope and limits
The CPU test used three classes and L2 regularization. All builds warned about nonconvergence because the step count was deliberately limited.
