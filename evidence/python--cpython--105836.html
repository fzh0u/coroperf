Issue findings

Local observation
Both bridge Futures reported cancellation while their Tasks were still pending in cleanup. On 3.10.12 neither Task had entered cleanup at the first loop checkpoint; on 3.14.3 both had. Stock debug traceback identified submitters while the handles were queued. After delivery, the Task traceback and coroutine origin omitted both submitters. The 3.14.3 call graph showed the same cleanup for both Tasks.
What CoroPerf showed
CoroPerf records the two actual objects passed to _chain_future. The Task snapshot links to its construction record, and the bridge Future links that construction across threads to launch_profile or launch_inventory. Independent Future and Task object checks agreed for all 16 observations across both releases and both launch orders.
Scope and limits
The displayed evidence is a controlled two-caller Linux replay on CPython 3.10.12. The same test exercises the checkpoint and diagnostics on 3.14.3. The fixture holds cleanup open for observation and then verifies that both Tasks finish cancelling.
