Issue findings

Local observation
In the original .NET program, a stack captured inside A’s constructor after Foo resumed no longer showed Main.
What CoroPerf showed
Comparing the original and instrumented assemblies, the async state-machine identity linked A’s constructor diagnostic to the call in Main that created Foo.
Scope and limits
