Issue findings

Local observation
Rows.Err stalled in 7 of 20 runs of the posted program and 9 of 20 runs of the reporter’s repository example, while all 18 control runs returned.
What CoroPerf showed
Four traces showed a writer queued before Err requested its read lock, and GDB confirmed on three instances that the earlier RawBytes scan still held a read lock on the same Rows object.
Scope and limits
Local Linux/amd64 replaced the reporter’s arm64 host and private database endpoint. Lock identities and scheduling traces came from separate runs.
