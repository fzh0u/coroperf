Issue findings

Local observation
After a successful loopback HTTPS request with a 1.70-second server delay, HTTPX 0.15.1 reported 1 day, 17:09:47.016604.
What CoroPerf showed
Independent clocks located the error at timedelta construction, where the first positional argument means days. Passing seconds explicitly corrected the result and matched 0.15.2.
Scope and limits
Timings came from uninstrumented runs. The original used a synchronous client. Task-creation evidence belongs to a separate async control.
