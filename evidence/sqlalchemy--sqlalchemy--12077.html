Issue findings

Local observation
After AnyIO cancelled a transaction task, the pool reported zero checked-out connections, yet the original PostgreSQL backend was still alive after a new connection opened and dispose() completed.
What CoroPerf showed
The hard-close stack reached driver cleanup that could itself be cancelled. Shielding close in 2.0.38 removed the old PID by context exit, while asyncio.TaskGroup on 2.0.36 allowed normal reuse and disposal.
Scope and limits
Each local process cancelled once, with an independent observer connection. The final PID check establishes liveness only at that instant.
