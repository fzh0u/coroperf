Issue findings

Local observation
Both official releases produce the expected 50 ordered rows. At 16 threads, Polars 2.1.0 reads median 36,000 feature rows and 37,000 label rows across 15 runs. A direct limit pushes down 50 rows to the source. A one-thread control reads 5,000 and 6,000 rows and preserves the results. Across both traced runs, the right input generates 9 to 10 more batches between the first stop writes to the left and right inputs.
What CoroPerf showed
The two bridge callbacks have identical native address stacks. Matching each Task's Arc at first scheduling and execution recovers two separate bridge instances. The same SourceToken links the slice write in record 1 to the merge planner check in record 3. The planner drains its left input before signaling the right. Its writes in records 4 and 5 match the bridge checks in records 7 and 9, respectively. This distinguishes the left bridge that already saw the stop from the right bridge that could still deliver batches.
Scope and limits
A local parallel-drain patch passes 96 correctness controls and the upstream early-close test. Across 100 interleaved runs per variant and arm at 16 threads, median total input rows move from 74,000 to 73,000 on aligned keys and from 60,000 to 59,000 on offset keys. Both ranges still overlap. The issue is open and excess reads persist. The stripped stacks retain offsets from the official wheel. The byte-identical text section of the original supplies the labels.
