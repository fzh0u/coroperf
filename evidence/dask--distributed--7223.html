Issue findings

Local observation
After a task on a single-threaded worker called secede, the affected scheduler left a root task queued even though the worker had capacity.
What CoroPerf showed
After the fix, the first handle_long_running call cut the queue from four tasks to three and all five tasks could secede before the second event. Disabling queuing on the old build also passed.
Scope and limits
The 0.8-second window measured entry into the long-running state in the PR’s local scenario, not recovery time.
