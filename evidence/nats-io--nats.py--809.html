Issue findings

Local observation
A broker HMSG contained its stored UTC timestamp. The 2.14.0 client returned None for that same direct read. Its standard read returned the timestamp.
What CoroPerf showed
The direct formatter received the real headers and returned the same RawStreamMsg that get_msg delivered. The exact source fix reads Nats-Time-Stamp and converts it to a timezone-aware datetime. All four clients preserved the payload, sequence and subject.
Scope and limits
The reporter supplied a schematic call and omitted a version. The unchanged upstream KV test exercises the assertion. The coroutine identity was measured on the separate sensor stream; its broker timestamp is specific to that recorded trial.
