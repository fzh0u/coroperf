Issue findings

Local observation
Three runs of the original single-task example retained Cancelled’s implicit context. With two readers at the same line, a parent nursery timeout surfaced at fail_at.
What CoroPerf showed
Matching each Task and coroutine to its cancellation and creation site identified start_profile or start_catalog correctly in all six captures when the nonresponding side was swapped.
Scope and limits
The public issue was a diagnostics discussion. The two-socket nursery was a local extension on historical Trio and Python 3.6.2.
