Issue findings

Local observation
A task waiting on an isolated Future produced a destruction diagnostic after garbage collection, while keeping a strong reference suppressed it.
What CoroPerf showed
GDB identified the pending task passed to TaskObj_finalize, and the Python graph supplied its creation identity, consistent with collection of an unreachable cycle.
Scope and limits
The original app was unpublished. The local test covers the lifecycle mechanism, and GDB supplied the internal GC path.
