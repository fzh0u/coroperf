Issue findings

Local observation
The baseline failed the proposal’s health-check assertion in 1.040 s and the proposed code passed in 0.039 s, and a separate chat conversion test measured 117.2 ms and 16.8 ms.
What CoroPerf showed
The baseline health task entered its handler only after synchronous tokenization returned, while the proposed handler ran during the tokenizer task’s suspension.
Scope and limits
The unmerged PR used in-process ASGI, a controlled tokenizer, and a substitute generation manager. The production workload, GPU inference, and container restarts were outside the reproduction.
