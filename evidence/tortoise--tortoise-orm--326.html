Issue findings

Local observation
The reported SQLite query failed before returning a row because Tournament did not recognize teamname on two old releases and the pre-fix commit, while both fixed releases and the fix commit succeeded.
What CoroPerf showed
Tracing showed that Team had the field and the root model did not, while the fix resolved the TextField on Team and submitted the final team-name sort to SQLite.
Scope and limits
The original example had one tournament and printed no rows. Multirow ordering and worker-task attribution came from a separate file-backed control.
