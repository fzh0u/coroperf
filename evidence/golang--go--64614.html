Issue findings

Local observation
The pool reports three open connections while the driver has zero live connections, and a fourth waiter times out. The first replacement dial is held while two subsequent opening signals enter the buffer. Go's goroutine profile with ancestor stacks and the dial stacks identify the same opener created by OpenDB. The execution trace records a direct wake for the initial signal. The later buffered signals leave no opener transition identifying either caller.
What CoroPerf showed
The probe records completed sends and receives on the same opener channel. The pool mutex serializes the sends, and one goroutine receives them in FIFO order. A dial stack with no request ID falls between the receive and finish of one opening interval. The displayed joins connect that interval back to discardProfile (records 0 → 3) or discardInventory (1 → 6). Withholding the receive-to-finish intervals leaves both samples unassigned.
Scope and limits
This is a bounded standard-library replay on Linux x86-64 using Go 1.20.6 and 1.26.8 and a gated in-memory driver. The fixture returns ErrBadConn through Conn.Raw and controls dial completion. The probe leaves the channel and its empty payload unchanged. Two independent batches verify 16 caller joins across both releases and both discard orders.
