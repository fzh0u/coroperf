Issue findings

Local observation
On Python 3.10, the reporter’s shield assertion failed on AnyIO 4.7.0 and 4.13.0, and only the targeted connection missed its cleanup acknowledgement in a two-connection test.
What CoroPerf showed
After the readiness Future was cancelled, TaskGroup.start called cancel() directly on the child observed inside the shield. 4.14.0 routed cancellation through TaskHandle’s cancel scope and shielded I/O completed.
Scope and limits
The fixed-version checks used the upstream release-event test and bounded loopback I/O because the shielded original waits indefinitely.
