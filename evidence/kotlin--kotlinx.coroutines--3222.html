Issue findings

Local observation
All four UnknownHostException runs logged just two fatal lines, while all eight NullPointerException and IOException controls logged 27 frames pointing back to MainActivity.kt.
What CoroPerf showed
The platform logger took the early exit at Log.java:447 for UnknownHostException and reached printStackTrace at line 457 for both controls. System records independently confirmed process death.
Scope and limits
Tested on the author’s demo and an API 31 image. The reporter’s device build was unknown, and debugger breakpoints paused the main thread.
