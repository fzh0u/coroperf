Issue findings

Local observation
On 0.13.3 and the pre-fix commit, the reporter’s CherryPy app sent two lines, but the async client delivered only the tail of the 642,810-character JSON line and parsing failed.
What CoroPerf showed
The same reading task reached a decoder branch that replaced its buffered prefix with the next chunk, while the fix appends the chunk instead.
Scope and limits
The original CRLF body was served over loopback, and each run was checked against its actual TCP chunk boundaries.
