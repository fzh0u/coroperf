Issue findings

Local observation
Three GET attempts that returned 500 produced one start, one end and no exception signal. A 503 followed by a 200 exposed only the 200 at the end.
What CoroPerf showed
The signals surround ClientSession._request, while retries run inside the middleware. Two explicit GETs produced two signal pairs, and raise_for_status reported one exception after the middleware finished.
Scope and limits
Loopback HTTP responses only. Transport retries and OpenTelemetry spans were untested, and upstream had not settled the intended semantics.
