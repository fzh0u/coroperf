Issue findings

Local observation
After flatMapLatest switched flows, sending through the old wrapper produced a zero-frame ChildCancelledException with debug off, while the new wrapper delivered 9 to the active channel.
What CoroPerf showed
ART traced the stale send to Closed.getSendException; with debug on, removing just two R8 rules changed the trace from zero to 24 frames and exposed the coroutine name.
Scope and limits
The Android Activity used version 1.4.2 and locally controlled callback timing. The original app and effective shrinker rules were unavailable.
