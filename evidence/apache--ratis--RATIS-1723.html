Issue findings

Local observation
After three increments, GET returned 3, but 2.4.1 wrote snapshots at indexes 2 and 6 with an empty cache. A separate state machine restored at 2 still reported 2 after writing index 5.
What CoroPerf showed
Disk readback and object identity traced the stale value to the example’s metadata cache, and removing only the cache update from 2.5.0 reproduced it.
Scope and limits
Snapshots were requested explicitly on a single member and a separate restored CounterStateMachine.
