Issue findings

Local observation
Two failing async matchers omitted their test locations, while equivalent assertions after a direct await showed the lines.
What CoroPerf showed
The debugger’s async parent stacks for throwingMatcher led back to test lines 3 and 11, locating the loss at the matcher boundary.
Scope and limits
Stacks were sampled at those matchers. Upstream tracked the location fix in #5104.
