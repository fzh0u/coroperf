Issue findings

Local observation
The original test timed out after 130 submissions. In a new three-node group, a follower reopening a database at commit 135 stalled in NewRaft while the other two nodes still committed new values.
What CoroPerf showed
The follower blocked on a queue send in restoreFromCommittedLogs → processLogs before replay wrote anything. With proposed change #730 it applied all 135 writes and caught up.
Scope and limits
Tested on v1.8.0 with real Bolt storage and a clean shutdown. The wrapper persisted the commit position after logging. Power-loss atomicity was outside scope, and #730 was unmerged.
