Issue findings

Local observation
With two CPU/Gloo ranks, rank 0 read [0] and [2], while rank 1 read only [1], yet both loaders reported two batches. Each of two releases overcounted by one in 50 of 171 rank configurations.
What CoroPerf showed
With split_batches enabled and even_batches disabled, the length calculation counted the final batch even for an empty rank slice. Applying the proposed length change alone made all counts agree with the samples.
Scope and limits
Sequential CPU sampling only. The reported main revision was unspecified, and PR #4127 closed without merging.
