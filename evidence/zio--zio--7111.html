Issue findings

Local observation
The original ZIO 2.0.0 example failed in Nil.head, but its printed trace omitted the map call at Main.scala:4.
What CoroPerf showed
The affected capture showed line 4 calling Nil.head and the path into Cause.die. The upstream fix retained lastTrace when FiberRuntime.runLoop handled the unexpected exception.
Scope and limits
Versions 2.0.1 and 2.0.2 were also replayed. Patch-release differences alone do not isolate one PR’s contribution.
