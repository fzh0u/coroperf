Issue findings

Local observation
One warm-up and 16 measured counter increments over loopback gRPC produced a final value of 17 and 17 JMX write samples.
What CoroPerf showed
Debugger object identity linked the timer’s start and stop to the same context. Uninstrumented client timings and JMX showed that the old start point omitted request-processing time.
Scope and limits
The replay used one healthy leader and the merged timing change. Durations came from uninstrumented runs. The debugger identified platform threads.
