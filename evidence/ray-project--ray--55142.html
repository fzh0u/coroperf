Issue findings

Local observation
On 2.48.0 the main-loop handle.remote call raised a cross-thread error with asyncio debugging on and returned the string “1” with it off, while 2.54.0 returned “1” with debugging on.
What CoroPerf showed
The failed Task, target loop and two thread identities explained the call-site dependency and allowed comparison with Task creation in the fixed version.
Scope and limits
The debug-mode error was reproducible. Its relationship to two reported production stalls in roughly 90 days remains unconfirmed.
