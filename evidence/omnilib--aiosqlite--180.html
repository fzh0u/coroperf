Issue findings

Local observation
The three original SELECT 1 examples returned Row, Row and tuple on both 0.17.0 and 0.18.0; the fix and 0.19.0 returned Row when requested on either connection or cursor.
What CoroPerf showed
The old wrapper held the setting while the worker’s sqlite3.Cursor still had row_factory=None, and object identity confirmed that the same tuple passed through the Future to the caller.
Scope and limits
The report gave no version or platform. Task creation evidence came only from an additional two-cursor control.
