Issue findings

Local observation
The reporter’s February row appeared in January group 2, raising its sum from 5 to 13. In another case an empty category took the missing-key row’s value, first 31 and then 131.
What CoroPerf showed
The combined ID for missing key −1 collided with a valid group. Restoring −1 after reordering returned the sum to 5 and made all four upstream parameter cases pass.
Scope and limits
The isolated patch ran on 3.0.0 with 3.0.1 as a release control. The Cython wrapper was observed, not the C summation internals.
