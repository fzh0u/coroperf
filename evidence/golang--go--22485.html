Issue findings

Local observation
Both historical builds took about 5 seconds to return from the original program. In a paired diagnostic run, setting WaitDelay reduced the wait from 903 ms to 271 ms.
What CoroPerf showed
The Go dump separated the parent waiting for copying to finish from the writerDescriptor copy goroutine. An independent /proc check showed that a descendant still held the pipe’s write end.
Scope and limits
The graph comes from the paired Go 1.23 diagnostic program. The two original runs and the descriptor evidence were recorded separately.
