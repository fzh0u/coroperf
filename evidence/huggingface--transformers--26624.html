Issue findings

Local observation
For GPT-2 prompts of 4 and 413 tokens, the old code divided each one-token generation’s log probability by 5 and 414. A three-token control used 8 instead of 3.
What CoroPerf showed
With identical weights and returned tokens, the merged change normalized by generated length, correcting all five candidate scores for each prompt and the three-token control.
Scope and limits
The replay used CPU instead of CUDA and evaluated returned scores. Early EOS and ranking across different completion lengths were not tested.
