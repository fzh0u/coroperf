Issue findings

Local observation
With the reporter’s await order, a Ray 2.47.1 request remained pending after another event loop released the lock.
What CoroPerf showed
The lock, ActorReplicaResult and loop identities located the cross-loop release, with reversed await order and a newer release as controls.
Scope and limits
Local Ray Serve deployment. The comparison did not measure general throughput.
