Issue findings

Local observation
The first response reaches the Ray replica caller in about 46 ms in both streams. On PR #67968 the interactive stream has about 5 ms below the engine boundary and 40 ms of first-chunk egress. The batch stream has about 33 ms below the engine boundary and 12 ms of egress. The baseline emits none of the proposal's five latency observations. Pooling 20 requests with one model tag, subtracting the separate p95 values for ingress and egress from the TTFT p95 gives 5.2 ms. The p95 of the request-paired engine intervals is 33.4 ms. A local patch measures that engine p95 directly and reports 33.4 ms in the matched replay.
What CoroPerf showed
Both first chunks enter an actual Batcher queue through identical execution frames. Matching the live reader Task, its Batcher, and the exact queue joins records 4 and 5 to their creation records 2 and 3. The request Tasks that created those readers join to interactive and batch launches 0 and 1. The exact first-chunk strings make the same trip from the engine through the queue and into each caller's first emission. Asyncio debug gives the common spawn path and omits the original launchers. Both launch orders preserve the matches.
Scope and limits
The replay runs pinned Ray LLMServer stream handling, Batcher, and the proposed Ray Histogram calls with a local engine fixture and a 45 ms batching interval. Five runs per arm and launch order use one shared model tag. Percentiles are calculated from raw histogram observations at the replica boundary. The tests cover that boundary and exclude model execution and router or proxy queueing. The proposal passes its 12 upstream tests. The local patch also passes the test that pairs the engine histogram with a caller's first emission. The issue and proposal are open.
