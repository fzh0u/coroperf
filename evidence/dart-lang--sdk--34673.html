Issue findings

Local observation
At optimization threshold 5, the original Observatory test paused at caught line 29 instead of uncaught line 34 in all three runs on each of two development builds.
What CoroPerf showed
The VM Service stack led through doThrow and asyncThrower to testeeMain at line 29. The default threshold stopped at line 34, and Dart 2.1.0 passed all three low-threshold runs.
Scope and limits
The parent and child VMs ran together. The inlining explanation comes from the upstream fix. The original CI build and failure rate are unknown.
