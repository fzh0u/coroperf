Issue findings

Local observation
Calling f on the reporter’s bytecode on a loopback chain produced an eth_estimateGas revert with reason boom and a CALL_EXCEPTION without the contract call line.
What CoroPerf showed
The Promise stack showed sendTransaction, sendUncheckedTransaction and estimateGas, matching the failed RPC method. The control bytecode deployed successfully.
Scope and limits
Ganache replaced the remote chain. The outermost call was not uniquely linked to the error, and upstream did not confirm why the frame was missing.
