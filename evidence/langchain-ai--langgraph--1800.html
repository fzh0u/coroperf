Issue findings

Local observation
The reported in-memory SQLite get_tuple call exceeded two seconds, while its async counterpart completed and the fixed synchronous entry point raised InvalidStateError immediately.
What CoroPerf showed
The loop thread scheduled the coroutine back onto itself and then blocked in Future.result(), preventing the callback from running.
Scope and limits
The replay used the reported framework and storage packages with a newer Python patch release and covered one checkpoint read.
