Issue findings

Local observation
After the public program suspended and resumed, NativeAOT printed ThrowAfterResume, Outer and Main, while CoreCLR included Forwarder in both runs.
What CoroPerf showed
External symbols mapped RVA 0xce097 to Forwarder at line 31 despite hidden ILC metadata. Making the method explicitly async restored its name in the exception text.
Scope and limits
The address required external symbolization. No Task identity was recorded, and upstream has not confirmed a root cause or fix.
