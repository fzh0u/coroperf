Issue findings

Local observation
With 20 jobs and 50-second callbacks, three processes peaked at one unacknowledged message and finished in about 1,001 seconds. Raising max_ack_pending to 3 gave a peak of three and about 601 seconds.
What CoroPerf showed
All three subscriptions shared one durable consumer: async sleep alone left them taking turns at limit 1, while limit 3 allowed overlapping callbacks even with blocking sleep.
Scope and limits
The broker supplied the pending counts. The report omitted versions, and a separate short-callback run measured overlap.
