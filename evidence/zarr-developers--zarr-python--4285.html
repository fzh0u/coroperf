Issue findings

Local observation
On a 4×2 array, indices [3,0] worked as int64 but failed as uint8 by selecting row 3 within a two-row chunk, while sorted uint64 indices also failed.
What CoroPerf showed
The graph identified the failing chunk-read task, and a separate probe showed a uint8 difference wrapping to 253 and falsely marking the indices as increasing, while subtracting a chunk offset from uint64 produced float64.
Scope and limits
Linux x86-64 replaced macOS ARM with matching Python, NumPy, and numcodecs versions. Separate value traces and fix controls established the numerical cause.
