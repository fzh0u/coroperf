Issue findings

Local observation
Two uninstrumented 3.0.0 runs matched the posted 11 batches, including worker 0’s first batch of [0,8,16,24,0,8,16,24] from 25 shards and eight workers.
What CoroPerf showed
Parent-process frames located the lost shuffle state at interleave construction, and worker records matched delivered batches. In the 5.1.0 control both copies retained the shuffled input order.
Scope and limits
The bounded replay used shuffle buffer 1, probabilities [1,0] and a CPU PyTorch 2.1.0 DataLoader. Task numbers and receipt in the parent established delivery order independently of worker completion order.
