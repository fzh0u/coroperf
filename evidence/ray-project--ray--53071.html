Issue findings

Local observation
One real Serve replica received 24 integers and correctly doubled them in three batches of eight, but three one-second waits took 3.05 seconds in total.
What CoroPerf showed
The batch queue waited for model I/O even with more requests ready, whereas setting the new concurrency limit to three let the three waits overlap.
Scope and limits
The reporter’s model used NumPy and a bounded sleep to represent async I/O. The new setting is optional and defaults to serial execution.
