Issue findings

Local observation
Twenty-four handle requests fill three batches of eight in one Serve replica. All 24 are enqueued before the first batch finishes. Ray 2.45.0 executes the three batches serially. In the uninstrumented comparison, wall time falls from 3.05 to 1.04 seconds with max_concurrent_batches=3 on Ray 2.48.0. All 24 results match.
What CoroPerf showed
The three batch execution records 11, 26, and 27 join to the same queue Task creation at record 1. Its creation stack is inside the first request's enqueue, with that request Task recorded at 0. The exact submitted request and result Future identify the eight members of each batch. In the fixed release with concurrency three, three separate worker Tasks start before any batch finishes.
Scope and limits
The local CPU deployment uses the reporter's batching settings and one-second asynchronous sleep to model waiting for the model. Forward and reverse submission orders validate 96 request-to-batch matches across both releases. Wall times come from separate uninstrumented runs. PR #53096 merged and introduced an opt-in concurrency limit. The default remains one.
