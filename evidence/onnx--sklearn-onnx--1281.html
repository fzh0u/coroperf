Issue findings

Local observation
Both reported dependency sets and their ONNX reference evaluators reproduced the preprocessing error. A separate one-level decision tree changed class on two of four rows.
What CoroPerf showed
The converter skipped selection when the column count matched the input width. The fix also checked positions and emitted an ArrayFeatureExtractor with indices [1,0].
Scope and limits
The replay used one known-width float tensor, integer indices and CPU execution. The classification example was a local control.
