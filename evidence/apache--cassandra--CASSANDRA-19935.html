Issue findings

Local observation
The conditional insert succeeded, but all 192 remote responses in Cassandra 4.1.7 returned three extra gossip entries, while all 96 in-process responses returned none.
What CoroPerf showed
The remote HashSet and local AsList contained the same three addresses; replacing only Electorate or upgrading to 4.1.8 made all 192 remote comparisons equal and removed the extra entries.
Scope and limits
Fixed membership and serial requests on loopback. TCP counts include background traffic, and messages were not linked across nodes by ID.
