Issue findings

Local observation
After successful HTTP navigation, a missing selector timed out at 500 ms and the reporter’s handler exited with status 1, while the matching local page printed Done.
What CoroPerf showed
At the timeout, V8’s immediate async parent stack contained waitForSelector and main on line 21, recovering the application caller missing from the error.
Scope and limits
The test used Puppeteer 0.10.1 and local pages. The 2017 external page and the reporter’s macOS build were unavailable.
