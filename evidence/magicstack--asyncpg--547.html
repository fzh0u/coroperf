Issue findings

Local observation
Both timed acquisitions complete internally just before their requesting Tasks are cancelled. On 0.20.1 neither request enters its connection context, and the pool waits for both slots after both acquisition Tasks and requesting Tasks are collected. The native Task stacks and coroutine origins lead to the common shutdown path. Future creation traces and tracemalloc show the same release-Future allocation for both.
What CoroPerf showed
The probe records the same release Future and holder at acquisition and shutdown. It connects the blocked Task to the acquisition Task, then follows its creation to the original request. Records 1 → 3 → 6 → 8 recover start_refresh. Records 0 → 2 → 7 → 9 recover start_report. Without the release-Future edges both observed waits end at the common shutdown path.
Scope and limits
The replay uses a two-slot pool on CPython 3.7.6 and PostgreSQL 11.22. It schedules cancellation after both internal acquisitions complete, while the requests still await their results. Two batches verify eight blocked joins in both launch orders and 24 successful close controls, including 0.22.0 with the same cancellation schedule. The 150 ms wait is an observation bound.
