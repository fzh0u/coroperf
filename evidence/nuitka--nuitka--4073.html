Issue findings

Local observation
After athrow into an unstarted generator, all three 4.2.2 runs lost make_exception, while all three 4.3 runs retained it.
What CoroPerf showed
Observing the same exception object and Task at creation and receipt narrowed the loss to the interval between injection and receipt.
Scope and limits
Two read-only application probes supplied the enhanced graph. The compiler’s internal branch was not observed.
