Issue findings

Local observation
The reporter’s one- and two-worker programs returned the two oldest startup records on both affected releases, while 2023.1.1 and the fix returned the newest first.
What CoroPerf showed
The worker’s RPC task scanned the log queue from the head and stopped at the requested limit. Reversing iteration also restored the time-filtered results.
Scope and limits
Local Linux TCP workers ran in separate processes. The two nannies shared a process and logger, so their broadcast results were checked individually.
