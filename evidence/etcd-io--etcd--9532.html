Issue findings

Local observation
Both uninstrumented runs of endpoint health -w json exited successfully but printed plain text, while cluster discovery printed one line per healthy member.
What CoroPerf showed
The old health worker printed directly, bypassing the selected formatter, while the fixed stack entered the printer, and all three responding member IDs matched endpoint status.
Scope and limits
The merge and its parent were tested against healthy 3.5.5 servers. The fixed printer’s main goroutine was not linked to individual workers.
