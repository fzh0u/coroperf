Issue findings

Local observation
The reported revision, released verl v0.9.1 and the migrated recipe at 654568c reproduce the same ordering bug. Seventy rollouts publish, the consumer removes 64, and six remain queued. Resetting before the finished Tasks are reaped stores 76. At a limit of 70 the processor admits no new rollouts, even after reaping. The reporter's patch applied to the release and recipe stores 6 in both event orders and delivers the next batch of 64. It passes all 14 CPU controls on both versions.
What CoroPerf showed
Two identical queue publication stacks join to distinct sample Tasks created by the same processor. The exact payload from record 2 was already consumed when reset counted its finished Task. The payload from record 4 was still queued and its finished Task was also counted. Record 5 joins the admission pause at 76 to the same processor creation at record 0.
Scope and limits
The replay runs repository dispatch, publish, queue, and reset methods on a single event loop. Local events control the ordering and stand in for model completion. The issue is closed as not planned. The local patch covers the legacy recipe. The replacement V1 trainer is outside the experiment.
