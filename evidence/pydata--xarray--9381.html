Issue findings

Local observation
The reported example stores six missing values as float32 and supplies the marker as a Python float. With xarray 2024.7.0 and NumPy 2.0.1 all six remained in the output. For the Elgin 5 S station in Arizona, 3 of the 29 daily minimum temperatures in February 2012 were missing. Counting their markers as temperatures gave a monthly mean of -12.328 °C. The fixed decoder returned -2.223 °C, matching the original station record.
What CoroPerf showed
The physical stacks identify two points in decoding. CFMaskCoder.decode prepares the missing-value marker in _check_fill_values. Materializing the variable reaches _apply_mask, where the reported build compares the float32 data with a float64 marker. The merged fix passes a float32 marker to that comparison. Testing the immediately adjacent representable numbers confirmed that both valid neighbors survive.
Scope and limits
The reporter used a dataset constructed in memory. For the station comparison we mapped the original missing entries to the reported float32 storage convention and read the marker from JSON. The data archive retains the original file, encoding step and daily outputs. Both measured paths are synchronous.
