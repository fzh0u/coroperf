Issue findings

Local observation
Across two CPU ranks, a ten-sample map-style dataset returned ten items in both configurations, while the sized iterable with dispatch disabled returned 16 or 12, duplicating items 0–5 or 0–1.
What CoroPerf showed
Using shard length 8 and local batch size 4 gave a zero remainder and kept the whole final batch. Neither correction alone worked across inputs, but together they returned the original sequence in all 56 configurations.
Scope and limits
The two changes were a local implementation of the author’s proposal. The Linux CPU runs used synchronous ranks. The report used macOS arm64.
