Issue findings

Local observation
The subscription callback received hdr2=val3, and reversing the two hdr2 entries yielded val2. Single-value headers and three distinct keys were preserved.
What CoroPerf showed
A 2.2.0 line trace showed the decoder assigning val3 to a dictionary key that already held val2, matching both observed header orders.
Scope and limits
The report omitted versions. The replay used contemporary local releases. Upstream declined a nats.aio change, and nats-core was a separate control.
