Issue findings

Local observation
In the original Scala 3 program, the exception on the right side of flatMap printed only c, while the left-side control retained more call sites.
What CoroPerf showed
Separate observations showed a’s callback calling b and b’s callback calling c, consistent with the contributor’s account of where caller context is lost.
Scope and limits
No sample captured a write to lastTrace, and there was no confirmed fix.
