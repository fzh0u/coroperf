Issue findings

Local observation
After a brief AppendEntries interruption ended, two followers that still recognized the former leader repeatedly rejected its pre-votes and delayed a new election.
What CoroPerf showed
The requestPreVote rejection stack and RPC records exposed an incorrectly decoded candidate address. PR #605 read it from the RPC header and eliminated rejections under the same interruption.
Scope and limits
Three nodes shared one Go process and communicated over loopback TCP. Different election timeouts made the lease-expiry order repeatable.
