Issue findings

Local observation
The reported version and the fix’s parent put both NaNs first for [3, NaN, 4, 2, NaN], while the fix and 2026.2.0 returned [4, 3, 2, NaN, NaN].
What CoroPerf showed
Dataset.sortby reversed the entire ascending index [3,0,2,1,4] before isel, bringing NaNs forward. The fix also used the nonmissing mask when sorting.
Scope and limits
Checked values, missing positions and label alignment on synchronous NumPy arrays using live CPython frames.
