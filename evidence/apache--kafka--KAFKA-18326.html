Issue findings

Local observation
After cache eviction, the processor that deleted B scanned both A and B from a shared RocksDB store, even though a subsequent lookup of B returned null.
What CoroPerf showed
In 3.9.1 the iterator returns A before consuming the matching store and cache entries for B. Swapping just that class between versions reversed the result.
Scope and limits
Local single-node KRaft, one partition and stream thread, and a 1 KiB cache. Bytecode probes supplied key comparisons. Production frequency is unknown.
