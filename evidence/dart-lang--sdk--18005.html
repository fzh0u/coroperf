Issue findings

Local observation
The original app on Dartium r34591 reported “Error must not be null” with exactly two frames for a valid GSM 6.10 WAV, while PCM and A-law versions of the same tone played.
What CoroPerf showed
The entry stack showed onFileRead calling parseArrayBuffer, but the later error showed only an AudioContext callback and _AsyncCompleter.completeError; the single submission linked the observations.
Scope and limits
The original WAV and Editor screenshot were unavailable. Its encoding is unknown, and no cross-callback edge was captured.
