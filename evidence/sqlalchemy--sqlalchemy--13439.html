Issue findings

Local observation
The author’s program read nft_id='C2' for a row whose stored nft_id was 1002. An independent database read confirmed that all 80 rows across both tables had been updated correctly.
What CoroPerf showed
A cache hit could override the column mapping by position and share its getter across executions. Scheduling it first gave 3/3 wrong results on each fix parent and 0/3 after either fix or with caching disabled.
Scope and limits
Python 3.12 as confirmed by the reporter. Uncontrolled timing varied, and some 40-run batches on the affected parents had no errors.
