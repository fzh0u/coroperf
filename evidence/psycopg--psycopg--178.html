Issue findings

Local observation
Both posted programs issued two unmatched DELETEs with a nonempty parameter sequence, and only 3.0.6 failed the final rowcount assertion by returning −1.
What CoroPerf showed
libpq recorded two DELETE 0 replies, and the runtime trace showed nrows or -1 converting zero to −1. The fix preserved zero and positive mixed-batch counts.
Scope and limits
The original cursor was synchronous. PostgreSQL 14.13 was used locally, and the verifier added a final commit for persistence checks.
