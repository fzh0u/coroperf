Issue findings

Local observation
After writing 625757527765811240, Piccolo 1.22.0 read 625757527765811200 from all three fields, while native aiosqlite returned the original integer each time.
What CoroPerf showed
The old int(float(value)) conversion collapsed two adjacent stored integers into one result, losing 40 and 41 respectively. Int(Decimal(value)) preserved both in either submission order.
Scope and limits
The replay ran on Linux. The original reads were sequential. Task origins were verified only for the separately scheduled control reads.
