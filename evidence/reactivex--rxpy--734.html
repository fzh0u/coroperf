Issue findings

Local observation
The reporter’s print_now returned a 1970 date, and a supplemental stream timestamped all three readings incorrectly and dropped them all at a cutoff set to yesterday.
What CoroPerf showed
The task graph located the clock read and conversion, and comparisons across two builds and two clock bases confirmed the effect on timestamps and absolute-time filtering.
Scope and limits
The report demonstrated only now. The stream was an additional Linux test on Python 3.12.3, versus the reporter’s 3.12.10.
