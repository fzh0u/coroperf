Issue findings

Local observation
The custom-queue worker completed a probe job, but a submission without _queue_name remained pending until a default-queue worker started and completed the same job without resubmission.
What CoroPerf showed
The worker’s ZRANGEBYSCORE read the custom queue while the waiter’s ZSCORE polled the same job ID on the default queue, confirming the configuration mismatch.
Scope and limits
The producer and worker were reconstructed from the report. The verified task origin belongs to the polling worker.
