Issue findings

Local observation
Ray 2.59.0 and the pinned mainline planner format the background batch on the actor loop. The queued completion for the interactive UDF runs after formatting returns. PR #67985 moves batch-format work to one worker thread. In nine trials per version with no artificial pause and two Arrow blocks per batch, the median from queueing the completion callback to UDF resume is 2.58 ms on 2.59.0, 2.50 ms on mainline, and 0.082 ms with the proposal. Both sums match on all three arms.
What CoroPerf showed
On mainline the formatter records 1 and 4 share the same Ray execution frames. The live transform coroutine connects them to separate launches at 0 and 3. The delayed UDF matches its actual Task along 6 → 2 → 0. The proposal's background formatter runs on the executor. Matching the submitted callable and its live parent connects 14 → 13 → 12. The interactive UDF joins separately along 15 → 10 → 7 and resumes before the background format completes.
Scope and limits
The one-process CPU fixture uses the real Ray batch planner and Arrow-to-NumPy batch conversion. It supplies synthetic numeric data and queues the completion callback at format entry. The displayed stacks use a controlled wait to check event order. Timing comes from separate unpaused trials with 16 MiB of input per batch. GPU inference and distributed throughput remain outside the fixture. Both the issue and the proposal remain open.
