Issue findings

Local observation
Clang 19 at -O2 generates an extra 48-byte heap allocation for Baz while Bar resumes in the await_resume variant. In the destructor control, the Baz header is initialized 24 bytes inside the Bar frame. The two requests cause six total allocation calls in the affected variant and four in the control. At the affected observation, symbolized GDB shows the same inline Baz, Bar and scheduler stack for both instances. Both original launchers are absent.
What CoroPerf showed
On the stripped ELF, the allocator's result in rax supplies the address for Baz's resume and destroy fields. Machine-code dataflow recovers the different active Bar frames from the resume argument preserved in rbx. The matching live creation records connect the two observations to launch_profile and launch_inventory. In the displayed run, the allocator returned the same Baz address twice. Withholding the Bar resume model or the construction history leaves both observations unattributed.
Scope and limits
The replay schedules two instances of the archived C++20 reduction on one thread. Linux x86-64 Clang 19.1.7 builds omit frame pointers. Capture uses event-triggered register and stack snapshots on an ELF with no app symbols or CFI, using no function-bound sidecar or recorded resume intervals. Debug names are added after building the address graph. Two builds and replays verify 32 joins across both allocation layouts and independent launch and dispatch orders.
