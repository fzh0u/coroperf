Issue findings

Local observation
After Grant(1) on a fresh three-node cluster, etcdctl on another member reported a two-second TTL with one second remaining, while a six-second grant stayed at six.
What CoroPerf showed
The lease ID and runtime worker linked Raft application to lessor.Grant, where the server enforced the minimum derived from election and heartbeat settings.
Scope and limits
The report omitted the version and timing settings. This replay used 3.5.5 and checked the observed policy. The archived debugger recorded the scheduler launch and the sample in order. Both records lack timestamps.
