Repair guidance and limits

This is one Linux/JDK21 component adaptation of a WSL2/JDK27 report. The process exits normally; no permanent leak, full Reactor subscription lineage, generic automatic thread capture or production overhead result is claimed. The selected catalogue remains at 127.
