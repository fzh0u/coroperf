Issue findings

Local observation
Two ticks ten seconds apart each printed a timestamp and JobOutcome.error, with nothing on stderr. Both jobs raised NameError in frak() before reaching the explicit raise.
What CoroPerf showed
The task trace followed each exception from tg.start_soon through _run_job to release_job: JobResult retained the exception object, while JobReleased exposed only the failure status.
Scope and limits
The report gave no commit hash. The replay used master from the posting date and stopped the example after observing the failures.
