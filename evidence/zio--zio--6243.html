Issue findings

Local observation
Publishing before subscribing caused the read to time out. Reversing the order delivered the same element to map, where the expected exception was thrown.
What CoroPerf showed
The result matched the reporter’s explanation that the earlier publication never reached map. The upstream test waited for subscription, and the maintainer’s delayed publisher also reached map.
Scope and limits
The complete failing program was unavailable. The upstream examples and local publish-first control were recorded separately.
