Issue findings

Local observation
With one POST still open and a peer limit of one stream, httpcore 0.16.3 raised LocalProtocolError on the second request in all three uninstrumented runs. The server saw only stream 1.
What CoroPerf showed
The old gate used local settings while h2 enforced the peer’s limit. With the fix, the second POST arrived before the first finished when the limit rose to 1,000, and after it finished when the limit stayed at one.
Scope and limits
Two loopback POSTs reproduced the protocol error. The reporter’s APNs application, credentials and 100-request workload were unavailable.
