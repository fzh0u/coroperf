Issue findings

Local observation
Across 16 of 16 trials on the reported build and the fix’s parent, an immediate progress notification preceded 24 committed updates. Resuming at that checkpoint delivered a newer write first.
What CoroPerf showed
The send goroutine delivered control revision 97 before an MVCC event at revision 87 on the same stream. The fix routed progress through MVCC so lagging watches could defer it.
Scope and limits
Event revisions were checked separately from response headers, which can match. The production client’s incident trace was unavailable.
