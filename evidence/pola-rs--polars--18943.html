Issue findings

Local observation
With every group key equal to 1, adding set_sorted put the cumulative sum 40 for t=1 on the first row, where t=4. A second dataset also showed misalignment.
What CoroPerf showed
Sorting within a group by t can change its row mapping even when the group key is sorted. The fix restricted direct expansion to slice groups, and 1.9.0 matched the reference on all 24 permutations.
Scope and limits
The report omitted an exact version. Tests used CPU wheels and unique within-group sort keys. Native stacks and Rust source identified the mapping path.
