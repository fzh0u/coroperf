Issue findings

Local observation
The reporter’s example remained pending in two local process groups. Across three snapshots one worker ran the parent with the child ready, while the other three workers were idle.
What CoroPerf showed
The parent waited in gather while occupying the only thread eligible for the child. Both controls that relaxed the constraint completed, and the worker states matched the reporter’s dump.
Scope and limits
Ran the public example in six local processes. The AWS deployment and private transformation workload were unavailable.
