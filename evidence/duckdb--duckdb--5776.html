Issue findings

Local observation
The reporter’s pandas query classified December 31, 2024 as week 53 of 2024 on DuckDB 0.6.1. Python, pandas and DuckDB 0.7.0 returned week 1 of 2025. In the December NYC yellow taxi file, 170,718 pickups on December 30 and 31 changed weekly reporting groups after the fix.
What CoroPerf showed
The native stacks follow both SQL extractors into GetISOYearWeek. For December 31 the parent divided a zero-based day index of 365 by seven and added one, leaving the year at 2024. The fixed helper recognized that the next ISO year had begun, advanced the year and reset the zero-based week to zero. Across a complete 400-year calendar cycle, 343 dates had incorrect results in the parent. The fixed engine agreed with Python on every date.
Scope and limits
The original report used macOS. These Linux runs use the published Python packages for the pandas example. The debugger captured the equivalent timestamp projection from a real SQL table. The taxi study is a separate workload. It includes 3,668,337 December pickups and excludes 34 records whose pickup dates fall outside the month. The C API engines read the timestamp column exported through Arrow. The Python releases read the original Parquet file. The observed calendar operations ran synchronously.
