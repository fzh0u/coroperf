Issue findings

Local observation
In the displayed Bun 1.4.3 run, both immediates resolve their promises before a 165 ms epoll_pwait2 call that returns zero events. Both await continuations resume after the same poll. Their ordinary stacks are identical. The stock Inspector follows the async parent to setImmediate and the shared dispatch, and both original launchers are absent.
What CoroPerf showed
CoroPerf records the actual Promise object when each hop is created. The continuation looks up the object it awaited in the same WeakMap. Its creation stack restores launchInventory (1 → 2) and launchProfile (0 → 3) even though the second launch resumes first. The resolve and resume checkpoints in the syscall trace associate both stitched stacks with one poll. Withholding Promise identity leaves both samples unassigned.
Scope and limits
This Linux x86-64 replay uses two immediate promises and a loopback listener. The replay sets the GC timer interval to 180 ms. Both 1.4.3 and 1.4.5 show the stall. Direct await and canary dac8e3b0f show prompt continuation. The probe records Promise objects at application checkpoints. Two batches verify 48 joins, including the canary comparison and both launch and dispatch orders.
