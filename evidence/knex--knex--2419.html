Issue findings

Local observation
With Bluebird long stacks enabled, both missing-table queries returned 42P01, but only the chained catch and rethrow preserved its application query line.
What CoroPerf showed
The logged continuations traced to test1 line 5 and test2 line 13. The directly awaited error stopped at Builder.Target.then and process._tickCallback, while the catch path retained test2.
Scope and limits
Upstream did not confirm a cause. The Linux replay establishes stack visibility, not deliberate truncation by Knex or the maintainer’s Bluebird settings.
