Issue findings

Local observation
In the reporter’s application, three replies streamed one character at a time each became a single paragraph. Sending an entire reply at once preserved its paragraph breaks. After the fix, the streamed replies contained two, three and three paragraphs.
What CoroPerf showed
The queue event ID and task identity link start_processing to _postprocess_message_messages. The old postprocessor stripped the newly appended newline from the caller’s message dictionary. The generator then continued from the shortened value. The fix cleans a response copy, preserving the generator’s input for its next yield.
Scope and limits
The original app used the Gradio queue and a generator with no model. The release runs used the browser files shipped with each wheel. Runs at the two source commits used the 5.3.0 browser files.
