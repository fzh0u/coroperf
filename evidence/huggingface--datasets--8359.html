Issue findings

Local observation
Checkpointing after 1,500 rows on the reported commit yielded 9,500 of 10,000 rows in total, missing IDs 1500–1999. The fix and 5.0.1 returned all 10,000.
What CoroPerf showed
Prefetch had advanced the source cursor to 2000. The fix saved the second table’s start at 1000 and its 500 delivered rows, so iteration resumed at ID 1500.
Scope and limits
The replay covers synchronous filter iteration. A separately reported map issue shared the table-prefetch mechanism.
