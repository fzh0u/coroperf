Issue findings

Local observation
The affected release and fix parent raised TypeError on the first batch record and left the topic at offset zero, while individual sends wrote all three records.
What CoroPerf showed
BatchBuilder.append passed inputs straight to the builder. With byte inputs it stored alpha/raw unchanged, while individual sends and the fixed batch stored ALPHA/encoded:raw, as an independent consumer verified.
Scope and limits
Used local Kafka 2.2.1, supplied a JSON-encodable input and corrected an incomplete log statement in the example.
