Issue findings

Local observation
DDC 1.24.0 failed to deliver the async* error to its caller while an independent timer kept running, while ordinary async and DDC 2.2.0 async* delivered it.
What CoroPerf showed
The Node 8 debugger observed onError, throwError and addError, followed by the cancellation callback.
Scope and limits
The replay used compiled output from the original example. Cancellation and error delivery were separate breakpoint observations.
