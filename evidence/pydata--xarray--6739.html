Issue findings

Local observation
For the values 1 through 9 with a centered three-point window, the built-in mean placed 2 at the second position. In 2022.3.0 and the fix parent, manual iteration placed 2 at the third position and returned 8 at the last position. The fixed commit and both later releases matched the built-in mean.
What CoroPerf showed
With center enabled, DataArrayRolling.__iter__ passed slice(0, 2) into DataArray.isel at the second label. Those two values failed the minimum of three. The fix passed slice(0, 3) and averaged 1, 2 and 3. An independent check of the window members on NetCDF data found six failing configurations per backend before the fix and zero afterwards.
Scope and limits
The reporter did not specify a platform or release. The local comparisons use a shared Linux dependency environment. The Dask controls use its synchronous scheduler; the reported iterator is a synchronous generator.
