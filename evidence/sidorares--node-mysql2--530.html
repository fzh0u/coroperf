Issue findings

Local observation
Two entry points queried a missing table through the same helper, and neither caller appeared in the affected driver’s native error stack.
What CoroPerf showed
Separate query promises linked the errors to readAccount and readInvoice, and capturing an Error at query time preserved both callers in the 1.4.0 stacks.
Scope and limits
The test used direct promise queries against MySQL 8. The pooled-connection variant was not traced.
