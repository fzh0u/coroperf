Issue findings

Local observation
In the same bounded 20-worker workload without cancellation, only five workers acquired a permit on CPython 3.10.3, versus all 20 on 3.10.4.
What CoroPerf showed
The releasing task woke a distinct waiter but immediately reacquired the permit synchronously, whereas the fixed version yielded at that point. The task created by submit_batch released and reacquired the semaphore. The blocked waiter had a separate Task identity.
Scope and limits
This checks the upstream workload without cancellation and establishes no general fairness guarantee.
