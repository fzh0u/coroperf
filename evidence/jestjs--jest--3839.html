Issue findings

Local observation
The reporter’s unmodified spec and locked dependencies printed only “Failed: SyntaxError” and stackFormatter, and the loopback service received no request.
What CoroPerf showed
XMLHttpRequest.open failed to resolve a relative URL against about:blank, leaving Jasmine without options.error. Changing only testURL made the POST succeed.
Scope and limits
The public reproduction used a local service. A separate Jest alpha.7 run identified XMLHttpRequest.open but still omitted the spec line.
