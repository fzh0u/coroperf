Issue findings

Local observation
The TypeORM error stack stopped at the wrapper when PostgreSQL rejected “select * foo” with SQLSTATE 42601 on that connection. The control query “select 1 as ok” returned 1.
What CoroPerf showed
Breakpoints showed the application query entering PostgresQueryRunner.query’s __awaiter and the error returning through the generator’s rejection branch, consistent with the upstream explanation.
Scope and limits
The breakpoints captured the Promise wrapper but supplied no async parent edge.
