Issue findings

Local observation
After restart and two normal leadership transfers, all three nodes reached committed and applied index 24 on three no-ops, while their snapshots stayed at 21 with 19 application records.
What CoroPerf showed
Raising the threshold to four suppressed retries, substituting a barrier produced snapshot 24 with unchanged data, and the fix stopped logging the no-new-snapshot result as an error on both automatic and manual paths.
Scope and limits
The production log entries, FSM, version and trigger were unknown. The local run verifies the logging behavior, not what triggered it in production.
