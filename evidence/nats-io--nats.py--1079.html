Issue findings

Local observation
All 200 keys remained present, yet 166 of 300 keys() calls on nats-py 2.16.0 missed keys and 65 of those reported an empty bucket while four connections updated 50 keys.
What CoroPerf showed
A zero pending count could enqueue the end marker before client dispatch finished, and one traced watcher had delivered 198 distinct keys at the marker and eventually received all 200.
Scope and limits
Counts depend on scheduling. The concurrent program used no artificial dispatch delay, and the initialization change was a local diagnostic control.
