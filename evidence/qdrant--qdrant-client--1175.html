Issue findings

Local observation
Writing 1,000 points took 1,567 ms in one batch with a maximum event-loop gap of 1,079 ms, versus 1,592 ms in batches of 64 with a maximum gap of 76.8 ms.
What CoroPerf showed
The upsert task entered model_dump_json before sending HTTP, and smaller batches reduced the longest pause with the same point count, implicating synchronous serialization.
Scope and limits
Client and server were 1.17.1 with dense and sparse vectors, integer IDs and a 128 MB HTTP limit. Wall times were measured without probes.
