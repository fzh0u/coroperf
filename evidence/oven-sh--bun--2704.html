Issue findings

Local observation
In Bun 0.5.9, throwing after await omitted the caller of bBroken from the error stack.
What CoroPerf showed
On affected version 1.2.4, pausing at the exception recovered cBroken and main. The same script included a synchronous throw for comparison.
Scope and limits
The original baseline used 0.5.9. The Inspector graph came from 1.2.4, with 1.4.4 as a version control.
