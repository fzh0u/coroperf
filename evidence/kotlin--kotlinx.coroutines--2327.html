Issue findings

Local observation
Two of the six original JUnit tests failed both with and without capture. All six passed after DebugProbes was installed.
What CoroPerf showed
Job creation and completion records tied the two requestSuspend failures to their respective async and launch callers, the locations DebugProbes recovered. The collector and an independent continuation probe agreed on each failed Job and throwable for the async and launch cases.
Scope and limits
The replay used the original compiled test classpath and covered these two Future bridges.
