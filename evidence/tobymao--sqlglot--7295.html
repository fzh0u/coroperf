Issue findings

Local observation
Full optimization of the reported SQL raised AttributeError on two 29.x releases and the pre-fix commit, while the posted single-rule script exited normally but discarded invalid SQL that PostgreSQL rejected with 42601.
What CoroPerf showed
The full optimizer tried to replace a missing predicate, while the single rule took a faulty grouping path. The fix preserved this subquery in both cases and still rewrote eligible projections as LEFT JOINs.
Scope and limits
Execution was synchronous. Probes inspected the expression tree and grouping keys, and PostgreSQL results were read independently.
