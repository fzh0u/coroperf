Issue findings

Local observation
All three posted examples—resolves, hasAssertions and assertions—failed without showing the user’s test line.
What CoroPerf showed
The debugger recovered line 16 for resolves. The two count errors were constructed during teardown in extractExpectedAssertionsErrors, while a directly awaited assertion retained its location.
Scope and limits
The original registration lines for the two assertion-count checks could not be recovered.
