Issue findings

Local observation
The original Go program left its last goroutine blocked on a channel, with exited ancestors absent from the ordinary stack dump.
What CoroPerf showed
Runtime trace creator IDs linked the blocked bar back through foo and an earlier bar to main.
Scope and limits
The upstream issue concerned diagnostic visibility in this example.
