Issue findings

Local observation
After two leadership transfers with one follower’s replication delayed, the original leader stepped down, won re-election and still rejected new writes after the follower caught up.
What CoroPerf showed
The affected run showed the channel waits described in proposed PR #708, where a blocked replication trigger could leave the transfer flag set. With the proposal, the first write after recovery committed.
Scope and limits
Three local nodes and five observed write attempts per failure. The proposal was unmerged, and the observation window does not establish a permanent stall.
