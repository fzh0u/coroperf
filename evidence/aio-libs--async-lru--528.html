Issue findings

Local observation
On 2.0.4 and the experimental branch’s parent, inspect.stack() showed only bar. Directly awaiting it also showed foo and main, as did the propagated exception traceback in the second example.
What CoroPerf showed
A cache miss created a child Task for bar, whose creation stack identified foo and main and distinguished its first caller from four other waiters sharing the result.
Scope and limits
The issue concerned logging, and the experimental direct-await branch had a separate cleanup error.
