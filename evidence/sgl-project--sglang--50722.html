Issue findings

Local observation
At concurrency 48 on one A100, median output throughput was 13,534 tokens/s on the parent, 9,419 on the affected commit and 13,500 with only the polling limit applied in windows with no other GPU process observed.
What CoroPerf showed
For 96 requests of 96 tokens, direct polls fell from 9,120 to 96 per run, frontend CPU time fell from 97.7 to 65.1 µs/token and throughput recovered by 43.3%.
Scope and limits
The short local runs used a 135M model. One-second GPU sampling and different hardware and concurrency limit comparison with the upstream 120B run.
