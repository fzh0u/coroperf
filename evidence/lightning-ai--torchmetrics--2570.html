Issue findings

Local observation
After ten updates on four samples, recall changed from 0.5 to 0.6667 on the second read, and three four-element ROC arrays became the scalar 0.625.
What CoroPerf showed
Grouped metrics shared the first metric’s computed cache. Disabling grouping or caching restored the readings, and the fix passed all 64 combinations versus 16 before.
Scope and limits
This was synchronous CPU evaluation in one process. Scores and labels independently verified the scalar and curve results.
