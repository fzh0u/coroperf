Issue findings

Local observation
As the live user-key count changed from 0 to 1 to 1 to 2, the old tool reported 6, 7, 9 and 10, while the fixed tool matched every count.
What CoroPerf showed
An independent bbolt scan found three revisions in the key bucket and six internal entries; the old counter incremented for each entry, while the fix counted distinct user keys.
Scope and limits
One healthy Linux/amd64 member and offline snapshots were tested. The six internal entries were specific to this initialization.
