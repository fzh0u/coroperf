Issue findings

Local observation
The real Play service’s /dump/fibers endpoint returned four WAITING system fibers without business frames.
What CoroPerf showed
All four traced to tasks that Dispatcher launched through GenSpawn.background, supporting the upstream system-fiber hypothesis.
Scope and limits
The replay used Play 2.8.11 and Cats Effect 3.3.0. The rule that omitted the frames remains unconfirmed.
