Issue findings

Local observation
In the reported Play application on Cats Effect 3.3.0, the stock /dump/fibers endpoint showed four WAITING rows with no frames in the four-worker configuration. Full stock tracing left the same rows blank. A separate waiting fiber had frames for the periodic dump.
What CoroPerf showed
CoroPerf matched the actual IOFiber passed to the stock monitor with that object's wait registration and construction. The recorded creator edge leads to loader.AppComponents.dispatcher at AppLoader.scala:32 for all ten blank rows across three runs. The traced periodic dump fiber leads separately to AppLoader.scala:36.
Scope and limits
The page shows all four blank rows from one original-app capture. Repeats use four and two workers on the same archived releases. The execution samples record wait registration, and the matching stock dump confirms those fibers later appeared as WAITING. The experiment identifies their origin in this application.
