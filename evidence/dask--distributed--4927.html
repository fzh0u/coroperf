Issue findings

Local observation
Five 160-byte chunks replicated across two workers occupied 1,600 bytes, but TaskGroup.nbytes_in_memory reported 800 and fell to −800 after release.
What CoroPerf showed
Separate handle_comm tasks handled replication and release for the same key and TaskState. The retained task group exposed the accounting discrepancy across those operations.
Scope and limits
The measurement covers array payloads on two local workers. A downstream dependency kept the group observable. The fix removed the counter.
