Issue findings

Local observation
The start was 11:16 in the trigger’s time zone, but the old scheduler chose 11:00, 16 minutes early, while the fix chose 11:20.
What CoroPerf showed
CronTrigger.next read the hour as 10 in the source zone and applied the target zone only when setting the field. Converting first yields the correct five-minute boundary.
Scope and limits
Fixed-offset and named-zone tests checked the stored schedule and cursor, without waiting for the historical job to run.
