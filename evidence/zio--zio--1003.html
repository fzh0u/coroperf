Issue findings

Local observation
On 1.0-RC5, two calls entered unsafeRun on different threads, but after work resumed on a pool thread both ZIO.trace and the failure Cause omitted the initiating method and line.
What CoroPerf showed
Matching FiberContext identity in the debugger recovered the application line for the failing fiber, and switching which call failed switched the recovered line.
Scope and limits
The original app was unavailable. A local two-entry program and external JDI probes identified the root fiber’s entry point. No parent fiber was observed.
