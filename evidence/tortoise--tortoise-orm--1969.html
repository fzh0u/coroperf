Issue findings

Local observation
After supplying a missing reverse-relation name, the posted query returned no rows on either database, while independent aliased SQL, the fix and 1.0.0 all returned 101, 105, 105.
What CoroPerf showed
resolve_filters deduplicated the two category joins by table identity, putting both name predicates on one column. Separate aliases restored the rows, including the duplicate.
Scope and limits
The report omitted a version and its model definitions conflicted at startup. Task origins cover local prefetch controls. The posted query ran in its caller’s task.
