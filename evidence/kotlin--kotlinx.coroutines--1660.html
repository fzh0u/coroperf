Issue findings

Local observation
After switching dispatchers, the exception stack omitted callThrowableSuspendFun in the affected tail-call example.
What CoroPerf showed
The method was observed calling changeContextAndThrow before the switch, consistent with upstream’s explanation that tail-call optimization removes its state machine.
Scope and limits
Tested with Kotlin 1.3.50 and coroutines 1.3.3, using tail and non-tail calls with debugging on and off.
