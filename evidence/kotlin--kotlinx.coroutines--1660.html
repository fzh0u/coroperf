Issue findings

Local observation
With Kotlin 1.3.50 and kotlinx.coroutines 1.3.3, the dispatch-switch example omits callThrowableSuspendFun even with stock recovery enabled. In the two-caller extension, both exceptions have identical stacks, and the official DebugProbes live dumps also omit both tail callers.
What CoroPerf showed
The Kotlin compiler forwards the continuation through the tail callers without creating a wrapper state machine. CoroPerf records the still-live caller stack at DispatchedCoroutine construction and reads the same Job from the throwing continuation on the worker. Across two batches, all 16 joins agreed with an independent check of the Job and original exception, including trials with reversed launch order.
Scope and limits
The displayed stacks are from the controlled two-caller extension. The original tail-call loss was replayed separately on the same versions. The non-tail source variant retained its wrapper with stock recovery. The stock comparison covers these historical releases.
