Issue findings

Local observation
The unchanged reporter timed out on AnyIO 4.1.0 and the merge parent. The landed fix and AnyIO 4.2.0 exited normally in every stock and traced run.
What CoroPerf showed
The outer cancellation loop saw a host in a shielded scope. Its sibling was in an unshielded child scope, suspended in wait_cancel with no cancellation requests at the observation deadline. The fix traverses child scopes and delivers cancellation to that same task role. The graph matches the stalled coroutine to the exact instance created by func.
Scope and limits
The watchdog observes 1.4 seconds of pending work. It snapshots the sibling before cancelling the process. Each timing is a median of three local runs on prepared CPython 3.11.
