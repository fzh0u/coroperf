Issue findings

Local observation
In four loopback runs, the pinned client makes three queue status requests and returns the same eight-frame video. During the 1.51 s polling window, the two affected runs give a separate heartbeat Task zero ticks. Both runs with the merged patch give it 30 ticks.
What CoroPerf showed
The matching Task joins the affected status GET at record 1 or 3 to the export or preview launch at record 0 or 2. In the repaired runs, a worker GET at record 6 or 9 joins to the exact executor WorkItem submission at record 5 or 8, then to the corresponding request creation at record 4 or 7. The same polling routine moves from the loop thread to the executor.
Scope and limits
Each trial sends one queued video request to a local Fal-compatible service backed by a tiny encoded clip. The measured change is event-loop responsiveness during polling. Polling duration and returned content stay comparable. PR #5200 merged.
