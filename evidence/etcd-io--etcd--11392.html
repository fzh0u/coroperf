Issue findings

Local observation
On a three-voter etcd 3.4.3 cluster, compacting through revision 5 reduced each backend snapshot from 15 historical records to 6. All three current keys were still readable. Every member reported zero additional compacted records. The 3.4.4 release reported nine on the same workload.
What CoroPerf showed
Go recorded the MVCC worker being created at schedule.go line 70. On that same goroutine the compaction loop deleted the nine verified revision tuples. The deferred update passed 0 to the Prometheus counter. The fix increments the local count after each delete. A mixed cluster replicated the same writes and compactions to all three builds, allowing the one-line correction to be tested against the original 3.4.3 implementation.
Scope and limits
The reported symptom was reproduced with the upstream Linux releases. Native goroutine creation and compaction operands were captured in same-tag observed builds. The experiment measures historical MVCC records per member. Each physical snapshot and scrape belongs to the member named in its archive path.
