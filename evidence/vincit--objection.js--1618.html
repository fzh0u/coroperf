Issue findings

Local observation
Two application callers disappeared from native exceptions raised by failing QueryBuilder queries. Healthy queries against the same PostgreSQL server each returned one row.
What CoroPerf showed
At the custom thenable boundary, the identities of the two query objects linked their application creation sites to the corresponding rejections in handleExecuteError.
Scope and limits
The links came from a dedicated WeakMap probe and establish query-object identity only.
