Issue findings

Local observation
Two PostgreSQL calls on a public Knex release returned 42P01 without their query call sites when tracing was off. Enabling tracing added the SELECT and ALTER call sites at lines 1317 and 1326.
What CoroPerf showed
The await continuations and their catch samples identified both callers. Tracing retained the driver stack in originalStack, and changing the pool from 2–10 connections to one left the result unchanged.
Scope and limits
The reporter’s SQL, app and tracing settings were unavailable. These calls do not establish a Tarn connection-pool cause.
