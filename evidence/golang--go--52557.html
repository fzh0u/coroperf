Issue findings

Local observation
Two singleflight producers call runtime.Goexit. All four readers remain blocked in the same awaitResult stack. Live goroutine dumps and profiles with traceback ancestors end at their shared reader launch site. The stock execution trace records both producer launch stacks and exits. The readers unblock only when the fixture later releases them for cleanup.
What CoroPerf showed
Each DoChan call registers its own result channel on a shared call object. The capture links that channel to the blocked reader and matches the shared call to the producing goroutine and its launch stack. Records 0 → 2 → 9 connect one duplicate reader to launchProfile. Records 3 → 5 → 11 connect the other to launchInventory. Removing the shared-call edges leaves both wait stacks linked only to their common reader launcher.
Scope and limits
This controlled two-producer extension uses Go 1.23.0 on Linux x86-64, with the golang.org/x/sync revision in the issue and v0.4.0. Two batches verify 32 blocked-reader joins across both launch orders. Normal-return controls verify 32 deliveries on the expected channels. The test uses a separate stop channel to release readers after the blocked snapshots.
