Issue findings

Local observation
The report asked for the median of one labelled region. With int8 pixels 65 and 70, SciPy 1.5.1 and 1.5.2 returned −60.5. With two true pixels they returned 0.5. SciPy 1.5.3 returned 67.5 and 1.0. Omitting labels or widening the pixels gave the expected values on every release.
What CoroPerf showed
The synchronous call reached _select from ndimage.median. The live operand stack showed an int8 sum of −121 before division on the affected release. The fixed release converted both middle pixels to float64 before adding them, giving 135.0. Boolean addition likewise produced True in the old build and 2.0 in the fixed build.
Scope and limits
The original report supplies two small arrays. On the bundled Ascent photograph, three of 64 regional medians were low by exactly 128. The exhaustive table counts ordered two-pixel regions once per release. The two backport module runs share the SciPy 1.5.3 binaries. The historical runs use CPython 3.8.20 and the reported NumPy 1.19.1.
