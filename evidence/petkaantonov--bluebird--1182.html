Issue findings

Local observation
The reporter’s nested-delay warning omitted its creation site, while 3.4.7 included an additional origin segment.
What CoroPerf showed
The Promise returned by delay had no _trace, and _warn received that same object. The revised release exposed the delay creation site in the original program.
Scope and limits
Node 4 lacked async_hooks. Object matches came from an observer’s WeakMap.
