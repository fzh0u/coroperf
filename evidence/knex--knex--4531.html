Issue findings

Local observation
The public route lost its caller during query compilation on both MySQL and PostgreSQL, while wrapper tracing recovered the builder call on line 22 of the published callback.
What CoroPerf showed
Each rejection mapped to a promise created on line 21 and passed through its dialect’s QueryCompiler.toSQL; valid bindings read back row 17, while invalid bindings sent no target SELECT.
Scope and limits
The published SQLite callback used replacement database factories. The private helper and exact PostgreSQL version were unavailable, and replication was not tested.
