Issue findings

Local observation
After one RawPut to region 10, tikv-ctl 7.5.1 showed region metadata on the leader and both followers but omitted state from all three responses.
What CoroPerf showed
Independent RegionInfo reads decoded Normal on every replica, and the corrected CLI emitted it; the client source change adds get_state() to the JSON field at region_local_state.region.state.
Scope and limits
Normal is the protobuf zero value and may be omitted on the wire. The fix-side CLI identified itself as 7.5.3 inside the 7.5.2 TiUP bundle.
