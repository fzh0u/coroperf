Issue findings

Local observation
With row three held on PostgreSQL 14, asyncpg 0.29.0 delivered nothing before release at prefetch 4 or 50 and delivered rows 1 and 2 at prefetch 1, while the other cursor returned all five.
What CoroPerf showed
Task stacks distinguished the initial batch fetch from the next single-row fetch, and protocol records showed a complete large DataRow could arrive before the application saw any row.
Scope and limits
The report supplied no SQL or versions. A local cooperative lock controlled readiness, and stacks alone could not distinguish buffering from batch delivery.
