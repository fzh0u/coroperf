Issue findings

Local observation
On SGLang 0.5.19 both cold operations time out in the reporter's component fixture. Each leaves one broadcast unread. Starting the receive loop before the operation lets both broadcasts complete.
What CoroPerf showed
The suspended control calls at records 15 and 39 hold the exact Futures seen by the receive Tasks at records 20 and 44. Those receive stacks join to creation records 11 and 35, where initialize started the simulated receive loop. Cold records 4 and 28 show pending Futures before the worker has an event loop. Changing only the two methods to the proposed versions makes them initialize the receiver for cold workers.
Scope and limits
The CPU-only fixture uses real TokenizerWorker pause, continue and broadcast methods, a local asyncio queue, and a simulated receiver. The proposed two-method change was tested for both operations in cold and warm conditions. The issue closed due to inactivity. The linked proposal remains open.
