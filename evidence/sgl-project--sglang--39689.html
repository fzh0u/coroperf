Issue findings

Local observation
In the reporter’s minimal example, pause and continue each timed out at 0.5 seconds on a cold worker and left one broadcast queued. Both completed with no backlog once the receiver started.
What CoroPerf showed
In both warm controls, the waiting control task and the receive handler referenced the same Future, while the cold worker had no event_loop at that boundary.
Scope and limits
The example bypassed worker construction and used an in-memory queue, simplified receiver and scheduler. It did not establish a production service deadlock.
