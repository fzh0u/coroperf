Issue findings

Local observation
The RC2 parallel-failure example printed zio-fiber- with no numeric suffix in the interruption message.
What CoroPerf showed
unifyInterrupt had the actual interrupt.fiberId but called threadName on FiberId.None, producing the incomplete name addressed by the upstream fix.
Scope and limits
The reporter’s MainApp and a 2.0.0 control were rerun, with the formatter’s input inspected directly.
