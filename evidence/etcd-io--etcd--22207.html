Issue findings

Local observation
Against the same healthy member, three successful queries with client 3.7.1 each printed RaftTerm twice. Version 3.7.2 printed it once, and the term was always 2.
What CoroPerf showed
JSON and table output agreed on the value. Removing one field-printing line in the client eliminated the duplicate even against the original 3.7.1 server.
Scope and limits
Tested one Linux/amd64 member rather than the reported Darwin/arm64 environment. Only synchronous printing was traced.
