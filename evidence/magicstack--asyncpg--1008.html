Issue findings

Local observation
Both posted functions printed row 1, but the 0.27.0 and 0.28.0 cursor runs failed with 55006 and rolled back. Fetch and the 0.29.0 cursor committed the row and new column, which survived restart.
What CoroPerf showed
After iteration, pg_cursors still listed a non-holdable portal for the SELECT, and explicitly closing just that portal allowed ALTER in the same transaction.
Scope and limits
The host differed from the reporter’s Fedora 36 system. Portal state came from a separate run. Client and backend evidence was linked by connection PID and logs.
