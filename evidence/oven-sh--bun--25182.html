Issue findings

Local observation
With a one-millisecond timeout on Bun 1.3.3, TimeoutError.stack was an empty string, while a healthy request to the same service returned 204.
What CoroPerf showed
Pausing on the exception located the rejection of the suspended fetch, but did not reveal where the timer allocated the DOMException.
