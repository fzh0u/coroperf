Issue findings

Local observation
migrate:latest printed the reported warning with a stack starting at TableBuilder.toSQL, and after rollback, the devices table was absent and the migration ledger was empty.
What CoroPerf showed
The graph traced the error from SchemaCompiler.createTable to TableBuilder.toSQL, and V8 breakpoints read an undefined callback both in exports.up and when the same table was compiled.
Scope and limits
The test used the maintainer’s invalid argument and PostgreSQL. The original migration and dialect were unpublished. The graph identifies the Bluebird task that logged the error.
