Issue findings

Local observation
The reporter’s predictions matched all six targets. After excluding target class 0, TorchMetrics 0.11.4 reported a macro accuracy of 0.6667. The fixed implementation returned 1. On held-out Iris measurements, the two retained species had recalls of 0.90 and 1.00. The affected metric reported 0.6333 and the fix returned their mean of 0.95.
What CoroPerf showed
The confusion matrix and per-class counts were already correct when they reached _accuracy_reduce. The affected average gave equal weight to three classes, including an absent class with a score of zero. The fix gave zero weight to the class with no true positives, false positives or false negatives. The predictions and the other two scores stayed the same.
Scope and limits
A nearest-centroid classifier trained on 120 public Iris measurements produced the 30 held-out predictions. A separate calculation checked every prediction, and a scikit-learn reference checked the scores. The same results held in one batch and four interleaved batches. When a retained example was predicted as an excluded target class, that class still had a false positive and remained in the macro average. Evaluation ran synchronously on CPU.
