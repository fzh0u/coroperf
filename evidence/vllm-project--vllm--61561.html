Issue findings

Local observation
The base launcher stops the frontend before draining the engine. Two already-active requests each receive a first chunk and then raise EngineDeadError. Both open proposals drain before stopping the frontend, and both requests receive their final chunk. The local cleanup change also runs remaining shutdown steps when an earlier manager raises.
What CoroPerf showed
The two failure stacks at RequestOutputCollector.get join from records 6 and 7 to the chat and completion fixture launches at records 2 and 3. The exact collector and error objects connect publisher record 4 to receiver 6 and publisher 5 to receiver 7. The publishers also join to their own output-handler creations at records 0 and 1.
Scope and limits
The replay runs the pinned launcher, client shutdown and receive task, output-handler loop, error propagation, collector and generate methods through two in-process ZeroMQ channels. Model replies and manager signal delivery use local doubles. Full worker processes, HTTP and GPU inference are outside scope. PRs #61562 and #61598 remain open.
