Issue findings

Local observation
The reporter’s JUnit test omitted bar on a direct call but retained it with withContext(IO) on both 1.2.4 and 1.3.0, and both paths threw as intended.
What CoroPerf showed
The direct call still showed bar on first entering barInternal, but only the continuation remained on resumption, and both releases passed their two official stack-walking tests.
Scope and limits
The report named master, and the closing comment identified no fix commit or fixed release.
