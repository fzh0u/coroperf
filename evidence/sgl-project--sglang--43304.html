Issue findings

Local observation
Moving a 25 ms fixture delay from before the router receive to the first routing send moves the latency between stages. In five runs per control, PR #45036 reports median processing times of 0.97 and 25.94 ms, respectively. The observed send-to-receive times are 25.49 and 0.29 ms. On the local patch, the new transfer histogram distinguishes 25.77 from 0.33 ms over Pickle IPC, and 25.69 from 0.24 ms over MsgPack IPC. Both requests keep their original token IDs.
What CoroPerf showed
The interactive and batch request Tasks resume through identical physical stacks. The output with each request ID reaches its actual Event in the frontend. Matching the waiting Event, Task, and coroutine connects observation 4 to creation 1, and observation 6 to creation 2. The two replies came from the same scheduler packet and passed through different detokenizer routes. This connects the measured stage delay for that packet to both application callers.
Scope and limits
The local CPU fixture uses SGLang's sender, router, Unix IPC, and frontend output dispatch. The scheduler payload and decoder output are fixture inputs. The local patch samples the same-host send-to-receive interval, including serialization and deserialization. GPU throughput and the reported production queue durations are outside this comparison. The issue and processing-time PR are open.
