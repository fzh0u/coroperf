Issue findings

Local observation
Both releases returned foo with two SELECTs, but 2.0.48 issued the second on access to bar.top.foo, while 2.0.49 completed it inside q.one().
What CoroPerf showed
The older loader rejected the Top-to-Bar path and fell back to lazy loading; the fix also checked inheritance in the reverse direction and registered the Bar.foo loader.
Scope and limits
The report specified only 2.0.x. The comparison used adjacent releases and synchronous Python queries.
