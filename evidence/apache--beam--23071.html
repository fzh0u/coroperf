Issue findings

Local observation
All ten inputs arrived, but the affected runner emitted panes of four and six elements, while the fix emitted five pairs.
What CoroPerf showed
Eight of ten timer writes overwrote pending timers before the fix, versus zero of five after it, while spacing elements three seconds apart produced ten singletons on both builds.
Scope and limits
The local Python DirectRunner used TestStream’s virtual processing clock. Java and Dataflow were outside the test.
