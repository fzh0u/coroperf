Issue findings

Local observation
With open PR #67572 applied to Ray 2.59.0, the median 600 ms consumer pause goes from 1,054 to 135 scheduling steps and from 600 to 279 ms of executor thread CPU. On master at 01ef5c0, the local adaptation goes from 964.5 to 5 steps and from 597.8 to 10.5 ms of executor thread CPU. Each version has 12 runs per arm. Every run delivers the same 131,072 ordered rows.
What CoroPerf showed
Driver records 2 and 7 have identical polling stacks and each reads zero bytes. A live ObjectRefGenerator matches the ready result from ray.wait to the driver creation. The Ray native task ID and TaskContext index also match that creation to the actor's own asyncio.Task. Actor resume records 4 and 9 connect to those exact Tasks, then to the distinct training and validation launches.
Scope and limits
The public iter_batches fixture uses in-memory data on one local CPU node. The 2.59.0 arm uses the PR's exact scheduler changes. The master adaptation preserves the newer output lock and resource accounting. Seven upstream regression tests pass on the adapted build. The displayed stacks come from a separate trace of unpatched master at 01ef5c0. The issue and upstream PR remain open.
