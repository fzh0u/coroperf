Issue findings

Local observation
With IncludeGoStackTrace enabled, coroutine.resume returned only the diagnostic marker, while a direct call included a Go stack.
What CoroPerf showed
At the VM’s threadRun recovery branch, the debugger showed coResume, callGFunction and diagnosticMarker, locating the Go call before the error became text.
Scope and limits
Used a benign diagnostic marker. The original embedded host and Lua script were unavailable, and the #539 change was reviewed.
