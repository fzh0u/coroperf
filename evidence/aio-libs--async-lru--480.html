Issue findings

Local observation
With 20 concurrent keys, capacity 5 and a 60-second TTL, all 20 computations finished but 15 Futures remained pending on 2.0.2 and the fix’s parent. The fix delivered all 20 values.
What CoroPerf showed
Each of the 15 KeyErrors came from a completion callback looking up an evicted key, while the corresponding caller was still awaiting shield(fut).
Scope and limits
Each affected build was run twice with an external timeout. Future identities came from extra probes. The callbacks ran on the event loop.
