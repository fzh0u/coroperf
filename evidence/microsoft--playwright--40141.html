Issue findings

Local observation
The original test failed its color assertion in a real browser, but the ordinary error omitted the outer shouldBeCorrect call.
What CoroPerf showed
Matching the actual step Promises across three error observations recovered the nested methods, and the upstream await change in raceAgainstDeadline restored the outer call in the native stack. The two inner steps had identical error stacks. The native IDs of their returned Promises identify separate calls on lines 18 and 19.
Scope and limits
The page response was deterministic and local. Step ownership was recorded separately from physical callback stacks.
