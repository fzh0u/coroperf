Issue findings

Local observation
The reporter's synchronous benchmark converts the same 360 tokens to IDs 100 times with 5,000 added CJK tokens. Local runs take 87.045 s on Transformers 5.9.0 and 0.018 s on 5.12.1, with all IDs equal. The isolated upstream lookup change on 5.9.0 gives the same result.
What CoroPerf showed
The additional coroutine workload offloads two equal-length requests. Their conversion worker stacks are identical. The active executor WorkItem connects records 3 and 5 to submissions 2 and 4, then to separate caller launches at 0 and 1. The six added-vocabulary hits cause 12 mapping rebuilds, while six misses cause six. Reversing launch order and swapping the inputs moves the counts to the corresponding callers. In 5.12.1 both counts are zero and the IDs are unchanged.
Scope and limits
The original timing is for synchronous token conversion. The CPU offload fixture checks caller attribution with two real tokenizer batches of six tokens. It uses the pinned Chinese BERT tokenizer and no model execution. PR #46323 merged.
