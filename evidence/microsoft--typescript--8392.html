Issue findings

Local observation
Running the upstream ES6 output made fun2 throw “miau,” but the ordinary stack lacked the full fun1 calling context.
What CoroPerf showed
A debugger captured physical and async parent frames at the generated __awaiter boundary. A separate Bluebird example exposed that boundary through a previous-event stack.
Scope and limits
The Bluebird example threw a different error. Its frames and wrapper-construction frequency were not established for the original program.
