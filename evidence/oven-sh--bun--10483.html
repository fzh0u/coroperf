Issue findings

Local observation
When the original module’s closure threw, the ordinary stack omitted its top-level awaiter, and a split-line control on 1.2.22 retained two named awaiters.
What CoroPerf showed
One debugger pause showed where the work was created, and another caught the module executing await pending, distinguishing creation from consumption.
Scope and limits
The split-line program was an attribution control. The two sites came from separate pauses. The original also ran on 1.1.2.
