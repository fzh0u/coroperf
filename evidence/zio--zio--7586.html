Issue findings

Local observation
When retries ran out on ZIO 2.0.4, the output pointed to retry and omitted the original failure site in foo.
What CoroPerf showed
The callback still received an exception carrying foo at line 14, but called ZIO.fail again at retry line 11; PR #7590 changed this to rethrow the original Cause.
Scope and limits
The published program was rerun on 2.0.5, with samples attributed separately to each attempt.
