Issue findings

Local observation
On Beam 2.77.0 and 2.78.0, duplicated() on the named Series [1, 2, 1] raised WontImplementError for keep=False and keep='any', while the DataFrame control succeeded.
What CoroPerf showed
The aggregation already returned a Boolean mask. Removing the extra column lookup and restoring the name yielded [true, false, true] for keep=False across three partitions.
Scope and limits
The failure occurred during driver-side planning. The successful control used the merged production module.
