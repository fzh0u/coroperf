How to read the evidence

The recorded join

Each join has two stack-bearing records with a shared instance identity and a recorded graph edge or audited runtime task ID. Parent and renderer-owner links have their own labels. Frames run from caller to callee within each record.

Two coverage measures

The join count measures coverage in the published captures. The lower bound of 206 measures issue-relevant origins reviewed in the underlying 505-issue corpus. A join from a different observation in the same capture may provide context while the origin relevant to the reported symptom remains open.

Reproduction scope

The affected example, a reproduced component, and a limited or unavailable reproduction have distinct labels. A case can show a physical stack or diagnose a symptom even when creation was outside the capture window.
